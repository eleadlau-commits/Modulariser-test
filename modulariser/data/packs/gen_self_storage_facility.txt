# self storage facility
people: Chris, Hannah, Mark, Lisa
## CustomerOnboarding | Handles new customer sign-ups and introductions to the facility.
t contract: storage contract
a register: register customer details
a orientation: orientation tour of facility
register > contract
orientation > contract
## InventoryManagement | Manages the units available and their status.
t units: unit inventory
a allocate: allocate units to customers
a update: update unit statuses
allocate > units
update > units
## Billing | Manages recurring payments and billing cycles.
t invoices: invoice records
a charge: charge customers
a statement: generate statements
charge > invoices
statement > invoices
## Maintenance | Handles repairs, cleaning, and facility upkeep.
t schedule: maintenance schedule
a clean: clean units and common areas
a repair: repair units and facilities
clean > schedule
repair > schedule
## AccessControl | Manages who has access to the facility and when.
t access: access logs
a grant: grant access to customers
a revoke: revoke access for past due customers
grant > access
revoke > access
