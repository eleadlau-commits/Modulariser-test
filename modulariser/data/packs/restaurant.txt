# restaurant
people: Ana, Raj, Mei, Tom, Lucia
## Purchasing | Buys ingredients and checks deliveries in. | why: food has to be bought from somewhere
a order: order ingredients
t suppliers: supplier list
a receive: receive deliveries
order > suppliers
order >> receive
## Stock | Tracks what is in the stores and fridges.
t sheet: stock sheet
a count: count stock weekly
count > sheet
## Kitchen | Plans and cooks the dishes.
t prep: prep list
a cook: cook dishes
t menu: menu
cook > prep
cook > menu
## Front | Takes bookings and serves guests.
t bookings: booking book
a seat: seat guests
a orders: take orders at tables
seat > bookings
seat >> orders
## Billing | Takes payment and closes the till.
a pay: take payment
t till: till report
a close: close the till
pay >> close
close > till
## cross
purchasing.order > stock.sheet
purchasing.receive > stock.sheet
kitchen.prep > stock.sheet
front.orders > kitchen.menu
front.orders >> kitchen.cook
billing.pay > front.orders
