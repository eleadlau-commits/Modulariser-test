## Listing | Maintains the listing of all available cottages. | why: clients need to know what is available
t inventory: cottage inventory
a update: update listing information
t photos: cottage photos
update > inventory
update > photos
## Booking | Handles the booking process for the cottages. | why: clients need to book
t calendar: booking calendar
a request: receive booking requests
a confirm: confirm bookings
request > calendar
confirm > calendar
## Cleaning | Ensures the cottages are cleaned and ready for new guests. | why: cottages must be clean for check-in
t schedule: cleaning schedule
a clean: perform cleaning
clean > schedule
## Inspection | Conducts regular inspections to ensure the cottages are in good condition. | why: maintenance ensures guest satisfaction
a inspect: conduct inspection
t report: inspection report
inspect > report
## Maintenance | Carries out necessary repairs and upkeep. | why: keeps cottages in good condition
a repair: perform repairs
t parts: replacement parts
repair > parts
## cross
listing.update > booking.calendar
cleaning.schedule > inspection.inspect

booking.request > cleaning.schedule
