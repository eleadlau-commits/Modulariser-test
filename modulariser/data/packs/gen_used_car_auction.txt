# used car auction
people: Mike, Sarah, Tom, Lisa
## Inspection | Evaluates and documents the condition of each car.
t report: inspection report
a check: inspect the vehicle
a record: document issues
check >> record
record > report
## Bidding | Manages the bidding process during the auction.
t bids: bidding record
a open: open the bidding
a accept: accept higher bids
open >> accept
accept > bids
## Logistics | Coordinates the transport and storage of vehicles.
t manifest: vehicle manifest
a load: load vehicles onto transport
a store: store vehicles in auction lot
load > manifest
store > manifest
## Sales | Handles the sale and prepares necessary documents for buyers.
t contract: sale contract
a sell: finalize the sale
a prepare: prepare necessary documents
sell > prepare
prepare > contract
## Finance | Manages the financial transactions and payments.
t invoice: sales invoice
a charge: charge the buyer
a collect: collect payment
charge >> collect
collect > invoice
## cross
inspection.report > bidding.open
logistics.load > inspection.check
sales.sell > finance.charge
sales.prepare > finance.invoice
inspection.record > sales.prepare
bidding.accept > sales.sell
