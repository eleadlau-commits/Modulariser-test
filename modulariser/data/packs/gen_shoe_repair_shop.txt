# shoe repair shop
people: Alex, Maria, John, Lucy
## Assessment | Evaluates the condition of the shoes and determines the repair needed.
a inspect: inspect the shoes
t report: assessment report
a diagnose: diagnose issues
inspect >> diagnose
diagnose > report
## Materials | Gathers and prepares the needed materials for repair.
t stock: materials stock
a select: select materials
a prepare: prepare materials
select >> prepare
prepare > stock
## Repair | Performs the actual repair process.
t tools: repair tools
a fix: fix the shoes
a finish: finish the repair
fix >> finish
fix > tools
## Finishing | Finalizes the repair and prepares the shoes for pickup.
t polish: shoe polish
a polish: polish shoes
a package: package the shoes
polish >> package
package > polish
## Pricing | Calculates the cost of the repair.
t rates: pricing rates
a calculate: calculate cost
t invoice: invoice
calculate >> invoice
calculate > rates
## cross
assessment.inspect > materials.select
repair.fix > assessment.report
finishing.polish > repair.finish
pricing.calculate > assessment.report
materials.prepare > repair.fix
finishing.package > pricing.invoice
