# car repair garage
people: Mick, Sandra, Ravi, Jo
## Bookings | Takes customer bookings and car details. | why: work has to come in
t diary: workshop diary
a book: book cars in
a quote: give quotes
book > diary
## Diagnosis | Finds what is wrong.
a inspect: inspect the car
a diag: run diagnostics
t jobcard: job card
inspect > jobcard
diag > jobcard
## Parts | Orders and stores parts.
a orderparts: order parts
t partsbin: parts shelf
t partslist: parts price list
orderparts > partslist
## Repair | Fixes the car.
a repair: carry out repairs
a testdrive: road test the car
repair >> testdrive
## Invoicing | Bills the customer.
a invoice: write the invoice
a collect: take payment on collection
invoice >> collect
## cross
bookings.book >> diagnosis.inspect
bookings.quote > parts.partslist
parts.orderparts > diagnosis.jobcard
repair.repair > diagnosis.jobcard
repair.repair > parts.partsbin
invoicing.invoice > diagnosis.jobcard
invoicing.invoice > parts.partslist
