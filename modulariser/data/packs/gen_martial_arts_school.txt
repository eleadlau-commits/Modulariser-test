## Training | Manages the training sessions for students.
t schedule: training schedule
a conduct: conduct training sessions
a assess: assess progress
conduct >> assess
conduct > schedule
## Grading | Evaluates students for belt promotions.
t criteria: grading criteria
a evaluate: evaluate students
a promote: promote to next belt
evaluate >> promote
evaluate > criteria
## Facilities | Ensures the training facility is well-maintained.
t maintenance: maintenance schedule
a clean: clean the dojo
a inspect: inspect equipment
clean >> inspect
inspect > maintenance
## Administration | Handles registration and billing.
t registration: student registration
a register: register new students
a bill: bill monthly fees
register > registration
bill > registration
## Competition | Prepares students for and participates in martial arts competitions.
t calendar: competition calendar
a prepare: prepare for competitions
a compete: compete in tournaments
prepare >> compete
prepare > calendar
## cross
training.conduct > grading.evaluate
facilities.inspect > training.schedule
administration.register > training.schedule
administration.bill > facilities.maintenance
competition.prepare >> training.assess
competition.calendar > training.schedule
