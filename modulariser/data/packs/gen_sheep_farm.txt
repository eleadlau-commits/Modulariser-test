# sheep farm
people: Laura, Martin, Sam, Emily, Tom
## Breeding | Manages sheep breeding and herd health.
t roster: breeding roster
a inseminate: artificial insemination
a vaccinate: administer vaccines
inseminate > roster
vaccinate > roster
## Feeding | Manages the feeding schedule and feed quality.
t feed: feed types
a mix: mix feed batches
a distribute: distribute feed to pens
mix > feed
distribute > feed
## Shearing | Shears sheep and processes wool.
t wool: wool bales
a shear: shear sheep
a sort: sort wool by quality
shear > wool
sort > wool
## Grazing | Manages pasture rotation and grazing practices.
t pasture: grazing pastures
a rotate: rotate pastures
a inspect: inspect pasture quality
rotate > pasture
inspect > pasture
## Health | Manages disease control and animal health.
t medicine: medicine stock
a treat: treat sick animals
a check: check for signs of illness
treat > medicine
check > medicine
## cross
breeding.roster > feeding.distribute
shearing.shear >> health.check
health.treat >> breeding.vaccinate
grazing.inspect >> feeding.mix
feeding.distribute > grazing.rotate
breeding.inseminate >> shearing.shear
