## Recruitment | Finds and evaluates potential new players. | why: the team needs the best players
a scout: scout potential players
a interview: conduct player interviews
t criteria: recruitment criteria
scout >> interview
interview > criteria
## Training | Prepares players for matches through practice and analysis. | why: winning requires preparation
a practice: practice match scenarios
a analyze: analyze team and opponent strategies
t schedule: training schedule
practice > schedule
analyze > schedule
## Match | Competes in live matches against other teams. | why: matches are the core of the team's activity
a play: play live matches
t lineup: match lineup
t strategy: match strategy
play > lineup
play > strategy
## Support | Provides psychological and logistical support to players. | why: players need support to perform at their best
a coach: provide coaching
a health: check player health
t playbook: psychological playbook
coach > playbook
health >> coach
## Analytics | Tracks and analyzes player and team performance to inform training and strategy. | why: data-driven decisions improve performance
a track: track match data
a analyze: analyze player performance
t dashboard: performance dashboard
track >> analyze
analyze > dashboard
## cross
recruitment.criteria > training.schedule

analytics.analyze > training.practice
