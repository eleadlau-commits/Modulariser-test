# market research firm
people: Emma, Liam, Olivia, Noah, Ava
## SurveyDesign | Designs the questionnaires for market research.
t questionnaire: survey questionnaire
a design: design questions
a test: test survey questions
design >> test
test > questionnaire
## DataCollection | Gathers responses to the surveys.
t responses: survey responses
a distribute: distribute survey
a collect: collect survey responses
distribute >> collect
collect > responses
## DataAnalysis | Analyzes the collected data to find patterns and insights.
t insights: research insights
a analyze: analyze data
a visualize: visualize data
analyze >> visualize
visualize > insights
## Reporting | Prepares final reports and presentations for clients.
t report: research report
a draft: draft report
a present: finalize presentation
draft >> present
present > report
## ClientEngagement | Manages client interactions and expectations.
t contact: client contact list
a meet: meet with clients
a followup: follow-up meetings
meet >> followup
followup > contact
## cross
surveydesign.questionnaire > datacollection.distribute
datacollection.responses > dataanalysis.analyze
dataanalysis.insights > reporting.draft
reporting.report > clientengagement.followup
clientengagement.meet > surveydesign.design
