# office cleaning contractor
people: Lucy, John, Sarah, Mike
## Scheduling | Schedules cleaning staff and tasks for the week. | why: ensures staff are allocated and tasks are covered
t schedule: cleaning schedule
a assign: assign staff to locations
a plan: plan daily tasks
assign >> plan
assign > schedule
## Staffing | Manages the hiring and training of cleaning staff. | why: ensures a competent and reliable workforce
t roster: staff roster
a recruit: recruit new staff
a train: train staff on cleaning procedures
recruit >> train
train > roster
## Cleaning | Performs the cleaning tasks at the client's premises.
t checklist: daily cleaning checklist
a clean: perform cleaning tasks
a inspect: inspect areas after cleaning
clean > checklist
clean >> inspect
## Reporting | Generates reports and logs of cleaning activities. | why: provides accountability and progress tracking
t log: cleaning activity log
a report: generate daily cleaning reports
report > log
## Supplies | Manages the procurement and distribution of cleaning supplies. | why: ensures that staff have the necessary equipment
t inventory: supply inventory
a order: order supplies
a distribute: distribute supplies to staff
order >> inventory
distribute > inventory
## cross
scheduling.assign > staffing.roster
staffing.train > cleaning.clean
cleaning.clean > reporting.log
supplies.order >> cleaning.clean
supplies.distribute > cleaning.clean
