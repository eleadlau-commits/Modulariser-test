# probation service
people: Lisa, Mark, David, Sarah, Emily
## Assessment | Evaluates the risk and needs of individuals on probation.
t file: case file
a interview: conduct interviews
a assess: assess risk and needs
interview >> assess
interview > file
assess > file
## Supervision | Manages the day-to-day monitoring of individuals.
t schedule: supervision schedule
a check: conduct spot checks
a meet: hold supervision meetings
check > schedule
meet > schedule
## Rehabilitation | Provides services to help individuals reintegrate into society.
t plan: rehabilitation plan
a program: enroll in programs
a review: review progress
program > plan
review > plan
## Reporting | Generates reports for courts and probation officers.
t report: probation report
a compile: compile data
a write: write report
compile >> write
write > report
## Support | Offers counseling and support services.
t record: support records
a counsel: provide counseling
a support: offer support services
counsel > record
support > record
## cross
assessment.file > supervision.schedule
assessment.file > rehabilitation.plan
rehabilitation.review >> assessment.assess
supervision.meet >> assessment.assess
support.counsel >> rehabilitation.review
rehabilitation.program > support.record
assessment.assess > reporting.compile
supervision.check > reporting.compile
