# school canteen
people: Ana, Raj, Mei, Tom
## Ordering | Orders food supplies for the canteen. | why: food supplies must be continuously replenished
a order: place food orders
t suppliers: list of suppliers
a check: check delivery quality
order > suppliers
order >> check
## Inventory | Manages the stock of food items. 
t stock_list: inventory list
a count: perform weekly inventory count
count > stock_list
## Preparation | Prepares meals and ensures they are ready for serving.
t menu: meal menu
a prep: prepare food items
a cook: cook dishes according to menu
prep > menu
prep >> cook
## Serving | Serves meals to students and staff.
t trays: serving trays
a serve: serve food to students
a collect: collect empty trays
serve > trays
collect > trays
## Cleaning | Cleans and sanitizes the dining area and kitchen.
t sanitizers: cleaning supplies
a clean: clean dining tables
a sanitize: sanitize kitchen equipment
clean > sanitizers
sanitize > sanitizers
## cross
ordering.order > inventory.stock_list
ordering.check > inventory.stock_list
preparation.cook > inventory.stock_list
serving.serve > preparation.menu
cleaning.clean > serving.trays
cleaning.sanitize > preparation.cook
