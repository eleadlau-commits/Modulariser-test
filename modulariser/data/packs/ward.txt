# hospital ward
people: Sister Ward, Kai, Dr Mensah, Ruth
## Admissions | Admits patients onto the ward. | why: patients have to arrive somehow
a admit: admit patients
t beds: bed board
admit > beds
## Nursing | Gives day-to-day care.
a obs: take observations
a meds: give medicines
t chart: obs chart
t mar: medication chart
obs > chart
meds > mar
## Medical | Diagnoses and prescribes.
a round: do the ward round
a prescribe: prescribe drugs
round >> prescribe
## Pharmacy | Supplies drugs.
a dispense: dispense drugs
t stock: ward drug stock
dispense > stock
## Discharge | Sends patients home safely.
a plandischarge: plan discharge
t letter: discharge letter
plandischarge >> letter
## cross
medical.prescribe > nursing.mar
nursing.meds > pharmacy.stock
medical.round > nursing.chart
pharmacy.dispense > nursing.mar
discharge.letter > nursing.mar
discharge.plandischarge > admissions.beds
