# phone repair kiosk
people: Alex, Beth, Carlos, Dana, Ethan
## Reception | Manages customer check-in and initial assessment. | why: customers need to be directed and assessed
a register: register customer information
t queue: appointment queue
a assess: assess phone damage
register >> queue
assess > queue
## Diagnostics | Identifies specific issues and parts needed. | why: to determine the repair scope
t parts: replacement parts list
a diagnose: diagnose phone issues
a identify: identify required parts
diagnose >> identify
identify > parts
## Repair | Fixes the phone by replacing or repairing components.
t tools: repair tools
a replace: replace damaged components
a repair: repair software issues
replace > tools
repair > tools
## Parts Inventory | Manages stock of replacement parts.
t inventory: parts inventory
a update: update inventory levels
a order: order new parts
update > inventory
order > inventory
## Billing | Handles payment and creates invoices. | why: customers need to pay for repairs
a pay: process payment
t receipt: receipt
a generate: generate invoice
pay >> generate
generate > receipt
## cross
reception.queue > diagnostics.diagnose
diagnostics.parts > repair.replace
repair.tools > parts.inventory
billing.pay > reception.register
billing.generate > repair.repair
parts.inventory > diagnostics.parts
parts.inventory > repair.tools
