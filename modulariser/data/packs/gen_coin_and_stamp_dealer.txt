# coin and stamp dealer
people: Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave, Erin
## Inventory | Manages the catalog of coins and stamps.
t catalog: inventory catalog
a update: update catalog entries
t ledger: transaction ledger
update > catalog
update > ledger
## Appraisal | Evaluates the value and authenticity of items.
a assess: assess item value
t reference: reference books
t tools: appraisal tools
assess > reference
assess > tools
## Acquisition | Buys coins and stamps from sellers.
a negotiate: negotiate purchase price
t contract: purchase contract
negotiate > contract
## Sales | Sells coins and stamps to collectors.
a list: list items for sale
t price: price list
a sell: sell items to customers
list > price
sell > price
## Storage | Safeguards and stores the items.
t safe: secure storage safe
a record: record item location
record > safe
## cross
inventory.update > appraisal.assess
appraisal.assess > acquisition.negotiate
appraisal.assess >> sales.list
inventory.catalog > sales.list
inventory.update >> storage.record
acquisition.contract > inventory.update
sales.sell > inventory.update
sales.sell > storage.record
