# christmas market
people: Sarah, Liam, Olivia, Noah, Emma
## Setup | Prepares the market stalls and space. | why: the market needs to be ready for visitors
a arrange: arrange stalls and decorations
t permits: market permits
a install: install lighting and decorations
install >> arrange
install > permits
## Inventory | Tracks inventory and restocks items. 
t stock: inventory stock
a restock: restock items
a count: count inventory weekly
count > stock
restock > stock
## Sales | Handles sales and customer service at the stalls. 
t receipts: sales receipts
a serve: serve customers
a sell: sell items
sell > receipts
serve >> sell
## Logistics | Manages deliveries and pickups of goods. 
t schedule: delivery schedule
a load: load goods onto trucks
a deliver: deliver goods to stalls
load >> deliver
deliver > schedule
## CleanUp | Cleans and dismantles the market after the event. 
t checklist: cleanup checklist
a clean: clean stalls and area
a dismantle: dismantle stalls
clean >> dismantle
dismantle > checklist
## cross
setup.permits > logistics.schedule
inventory.restock > sales.sell
logistics.deliver > inventory.stock
sales.sell >> cleanup.clean
setup.arrange > cleanup.dismantle
