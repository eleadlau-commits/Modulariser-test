# sailing club
people: Alex, Beth, Carlos, Diana, Ethan
## Membership | Manages club members and their participation.
t roster: member roster
a register: register new members
a renew: renew membership
register > roster
renew > roster
## Training | Conducts training sessions for members.
t schedule: training schedule
a teach: teach sailing classes
a evaluate: evaluate skill levels
teach > schedule
evaluate > schedule
## Boats | Manages the club boats and their maintenance.
t fleet: boat fleet
a maintain: perform maintenance
t log: maintenance log
maintain > log
maintain > fleet
## Scheduling | Organizes club events and sailing slots.
t calendar: event calendar
a book: book sailing slots
a notify: send out notifications
book > calendar
notify > calendar
## Safety | Ensures safety practices and procedures are followed.
t checklist: safety checklist
a inspect: inspect boats and equipment
a brief: brief members on safety
inspect > checklist
brief > checklist
## cross
membership.register > training.schedule
training.teach >> safety.brief
boats.maintain > scheduling.calendar
scheduling.book >> boats.fleet
safety.inspect > boats.log
