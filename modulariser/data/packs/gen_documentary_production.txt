# documentary production
people: Jake, Emily, Lisa, Mark, Sandra
## Research | Investigates and gathers information on the documentary topic.
t outline: research outline
a interview: conduct interviews
t sources: interview sources
interview > sources
interview >> outline
## Scriptwriting | Writes the script and narrative for the documentary.
t script: script draft
a structure: structure the narrative
a write: write scenes
structure >> write
write > script
## Filming | Captures the visual elements of the documentary.
t footage: raw footage
a shoot: film scenes
a direct: direct on set
shoot >> direct
direct > footage
## Editing | Assembles the footage and narrative into a cohesive documentary.
t cut: final cut
a assemble: assemble scenes
a polish: polish the final cut
assemble >> polish
polish > cut
## Music & Sound | Adds music and sound design to enhance the documentary.
t track: original music track
a mix: mix audio
a score: write the score
score >> track
mix > track
## Distribution | Prepares and distributes the documentary to audiences.
t release: release schedule
a package: package for distribution
a market: market the documentary
package >> market
market > release
## cross
research.outline > scriptwriting.script
filming.direct > editing.assemble
scriptwriting.write > filming.shoot
editing.polish > music.score
distribution.package > music.mix
scriptwriting.script > filming.shoot
