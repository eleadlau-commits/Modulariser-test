# chocolate shop
people: Emma, Lucas, Olivia, Noah, Isabella
## Sourcing | Selects and orders cocoa beans and other ingredients. | why: necessary for production
a select: select bean varieties
t suppliers: supplier contacts
a order: place orders
select >> order
order > suppliers
## Production | Mixes ingredients and molds chocolates. | why: makes the final product
t recipe: production recipe
a mix: mix ingredients
a mold: mold chocolates
a cool: cool chocolates
mix > recipe
mix >> mold
mold >> cool
## Quality | Inspects and tests chocolate products. | why: ensures product quality
a inspect: visually inspect
t sample: quality samples
a taste: taste test
inspect > sample
inspect >> taste
## Packaging | Wraps and boxes chocolates for sale. | why: prepares product for sale
t design: packaging design
a wrap: wrap chocolates
a box: box chocolates
wrap > design
wrap >> box
## Sales | Tracks sales and manages inventory. | why: keeps track of stock and sales
t inventory: inventory levels
a track: track daily sales
track > inventory
## cross
sourcing.order > production.recipe
production.cool > quality.inspect
quality.sample > packaging.wrap
packaging.box > sales.track
