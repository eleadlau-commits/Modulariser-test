# magazine
people: Emily, Jordan, Taylor, Casey, Olivia
## Content Creation | Writes and edits articles. | why: content needs to be created for the magazine
a write: write articles
a edit: edit submitted articles
t drafts: draft files
write >> edit
edit > drafts
## Design | Designs the layout and selects images. | why: visual appeal is crucial for magazine
a layout: design article layout
a select: select images for articles
t template: design templates
layout > template
select > template
## Printing | Prepares and sends for printing. | why: physical copies need to be produced
a prepare: prepare for printing
a print: send to print
t proof: print proof
prepare >> print
print > proof
## Distribution | Distributes physical copies and manages subscriptions. | why: magazines need to reach readers
a deliver: deliver physical copies
a manage: manage subscriptions
t delivery: delivery list
deliver > delivery
manage > delivery
## Digital | Manages the online presence and subscriptions. | why: digital presence is essential for engagement
a publish: publish online
a update: update website
t online: online content
publish > online
update > online
## cross
content.write > design.template
content.edit > design.template
printing.print > distribution.delivery
digital.publish > distribution.manage
digital.update > content.drafts
design.template > printing.prepare
content.drafts > digital.online
