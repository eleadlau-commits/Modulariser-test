## Requisition | Agrees the role and the budget for it. | why: a role must be approved before hiring
a approve: get headcount approval
t jd: job description
approve >> jd
## Sourcing | Finds candidates.
a advertise: advertise the role
a referrals: collect referrals
t pipeline: candidate tracker
advertise > pipeline
referrals > pipeline
## Interviewing | Screens and interviews candidates.
a screen: phone screen candidates
a panel: run panel interviews
t scorecard: interview scorecard
screen >> panel
panel > scorecard
## Offer | Makes and negotiates the offer.
a offer: make the offer
t salary: salary bands
a references: check references
offer > salary
references >> offer
## Onboarding | Gets the new hire started.
a laptop: order a laptop
a induction: run induction
t checklist: onboarding checklist
induction > checklist
## cross
sourcing.advertise > requisition.jd
interviewing.screen > sourcing.pipeline
interviewing.scorecard > requisition.jd
offer.offer > interviewing.scorecard
offer.offer >> onboarding.laptop
requisition.approve > offer.salary
