# mosque community centre
people: Fatima, Omar, Aisha, Ali
## Programming | Plans and schedules events and activities.
t calendar: event calendar
a schedule: schedule events
t theme: monthly theme
schedule > calendar
schedule > theme
## Outreach | Communicates events to the community.
a announce: announce events on social media
t flyer: event flyer
a distribute: distribute flyers in the community
announce > flyer
announce >> distribute
## Facilities | Manages the building and its maintenance.
t checklist: maintenance checklist
a inspect: inspect the facilities
t log: maintenance log
inspect > checklist
inspect > log
## Worship | Organizes and conducts regular prayers and services.
t schedule: prayer schedule
a lead: lead prayers
t call: call to prayer
lead > schedule
lead > call
## Education | Provides classes and workshops for various skills.
t curriculum: educational curriculum
a teach: teach classes
t resource: educational resource
teach > curriculum
teach > resource
## cross
programming.schedule > worship.schedule
programming.schedule > education.curriculum
facilities.inspect > programming.calendar
outreach.announce > programming.calendar
education.teach >> worship.lead
programming.theme > outreach.flyer
