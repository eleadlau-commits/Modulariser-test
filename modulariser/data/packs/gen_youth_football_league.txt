## Registration | Manages player and team sign-ups.

t fees: registration fees
register > roster

## Scheduling | Sets up the match calendar and venues.
t calendar: match calendar
a assign: assign venues
t schedule: match schedule
assign > calendar
schedule > calendar
## Refereeing | Assigns referees and conducts training.
t roster: referee roster
a assign: assign referees
t training: referee training
assign > roster
assign >> training
## Matchday | Oversees match events and reports.
t report: match report
a supervise: supervise matches
t score: match score
supervise > report
supervise > score
## Communication | Sends out updates and reminders.
t email: email list
a send: send reminders
t updates: league updates
send > email
send > updates
## cross
