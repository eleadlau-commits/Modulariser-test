# conference organising
people: Rhea, Kwame, Olga, Finn
## Programme | Chooses talks and builds the agenda. | why: there must be content
a cfp: run the call for papers
a reviewtalks: review submissions
t agenda: agenda
cfp >> reviewtalks
reviewtalks > agenda
## Registration | Sells tickets and tracks attendees.
t attendees: attendee list
a tickets: sell tickets
a badges: print badges
tickets > attendees
badges > attendees
## Venue | Books rooms and AV.
a rooms: book rooms
t floorplan: floor plan
a av: arrange AV
rooms > floorplan
## Sponsors | Finds and serves sponsors.
a pitch: pitch sponsors
t packages: sponsor packages
pitch > packages
## Speakers | Looks after speakers.
a travel: book speaker travel
t briefing: speaker briefing
## cross
venue.rooms > programme.agenda
speakers.briefing > programme.agenda
speakers.travel > programme.agenda
registration.badges > sponsors.packages
sponsors.packages > venue.floorplan
venue.av > programme.agenda
