# photography studio
people: Lisa, Mark, Emma, Ryan
## Scheduling | Manages and coordinates booking and consultations.
t bookings: booking calendar
a consult: conduct client consultations
a schedule: schedule photo sessions
consult >> schedule
schedule > bookings
## Photography | Captures the actual photographs.
t equipment: photography equipment
a shoot: take photos
a setup: set up the shoot location
shoot >> setup
shoot > equipment
## Editing | Edits the photos post-shoot.
t software: editing software
a edit: edit photos
a review: review and refine edits
edit >> review
review > software
## Sales | Handles sales and client interaction post-editing.
t portfolio: photo portfolio
a present: present the portfolio to client
a finalize: finalize client selection
present >> finalize
finalize > portfolio
## Storage | Archives and manages storage of photos.
t drive: external drive
t cloud: cloud storage
a backup: back up photos to drive
a upload: upload to cloud storage
backup > drive
upload > cloud
## cross
scheduling.schedule > photography.setup
scheduling.bookings > sales.present
photography.shoot > editing.edit
editing.review > sales.finalize
sales.finalize > storage.backup
editing.edit >> storage.upload
