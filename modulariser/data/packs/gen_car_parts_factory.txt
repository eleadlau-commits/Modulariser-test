# car parts factory
people: John, Sarah, Mike, Lisa, Charlie
## Procurement | Orders raw materials and checks deliveries. | why: raw materials are needed for production
a order: place orders for materials
t suppliers: supplier list
a receive: check deliveries
order > suppliers
order >> receive
## Inventory | Manages storage and tracking of materials and parts. 
t stock: inventory list
a count: weekly stock count
count > stock
## Assembly | Assembles parts into components.
t blueprint: assembly blueprint
a assemble: assemble parts
t component: assembled component
assemble > blueprint
assemble > component
## Quality Control | Ensures parts meet quality standards. 
a inspect: inspect parts
t checklist: quality checklist
inspect > checklist
## Packaging | Prepares components for shipment.
a package: package components
t label: shipping label
package > label
## Shipments | Manages the shipping of components to clients.
a ship: ship components
t manifest: shipping manifest
ship > manifest
## cross
procurement.order > inventory.stock
procurement.receive > inventory.stock
assembly.assemble > quality.inspect
assembly.component > packaging.package
packaging.package >> shipments.ship
quality.inspect > assembly.component
inventory.stock > assembly.blueprint
