# archaeological dig
people: Alice, Bob, Charlie, Dana, Ethan
## Excavation | Unearths and documents artifacts from the site. | why: uncovering history requires physical digging
a dig: conduct excavations
t trench: excavation trenches
a record: record findings
dig >> record
dig > trench
## Survey | Maps the site and locates potential excavation areas. | why: to plan where to dig
t map: site map
a locate: locate potential areas
a measure: measure dimensions of the site
measure > map
locate > map
## Cataloging | Catalogs and labels artifacts for identification and record-keeping. | why: keeping track of artifacts is essential
t catalog: artifact catalog
a label: label artifacts
a input: input data into the catalog
label >> input
input > catalog
## Analysis | Analyzes artifacts to understand their historical significance. | why: to interpret the findings
t report: analysis report
a study: study artifacts
a document: document findings
study >> document
document > report
## Conservation | Preserves artifacts for future study and display. | why: to safeguard the artifacts for future generations
a clean: clean artifacts
t storage: storage solutions
a store: store artifacts
clean >> store
store > storage
## cross
excavation.dig > survey.map
cataloging.label > analysis.study
analysis.report > conservation.clean
excavation.record > cataloging.input
survey.measure > excavation.trench
