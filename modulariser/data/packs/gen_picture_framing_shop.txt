# picture framing shop
people: Emily, Jake, Sarah, Mark
## Consultation | Advises clients on framing options.
a discuss: discuss framing options with clients
t options: framing options
a recommend: recommend suitable framing materials
discuss >> recommend
recommend > options
## Design | Creates the design for the framed artwork.
a layout: layout the frame design
t sample: design sample
a approve: client approves the design
layout >> approve
layout > sample
## Cutting | Cuts the frame and matting materials to size.
a measure: measure the artwork
t tools: cutting tools
a cut: cut the frame and matting materials
measure >> cut
cut > tools
## Assembly | Assembles the frame and mounts the artwork.
t materials: framing materials
a assemble: assemble the frame
a mount: mount the artwork
assemble >> mount
assemble > materials
## Finishing | Adds final touches and prepares for delivery.
a polish: polish the frame
t accessories: final accessories
a wrap: wrap the finished piece
polish >> wrap
wrap > accessories
## cross
consultation.recommend > design.layout
cutting.cut > assembly.assemble
design.approve >> cutting.measure
assembly.mount >> finishing.polish
finishing.wrap > consultation.options
