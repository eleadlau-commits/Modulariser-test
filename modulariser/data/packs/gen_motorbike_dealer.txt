# motorbike dealer
people: Lisa, Mark, Emily, Kevin
## Sales | Handles customer inquiries and closes deals.
t inventory: sales inventory list
a show: show bikes to customers
a close: close the sale
a finance: arrange financing options
show > inventory
show >> close
finance >> close
## Service | Manages maintenance, repairs, and customer service.
t schedule: service schedule
a diagnose: diagnose bike issues
a repair: perform repairs
a maintain: perform maintenance
diagnose >> repair
diagnose >> maintain
repair > schedule
maintain > schedule
## Parts | Orders and stocks parts for repairs and maintenance.
a order: order parts
t stock: parts stock list
a receive: receive parts deliveries
order > stock
order >> receive
## Accounting | Tracks sales, expenses, and manages payments.
t ledger: sales ledger
a invoice: generate invoices
a pay: manage payments
invoice > ledger
pay > ledger
## Marketing | Promotes the dealer and attracts customers.
t campaign: marketing campaign plan
a promote: run promotional activities
a advertise: place ads in media
promote > campaign
advertise > campaign
## cross
sales.show > parts.stock
sales.close > accounting.ledger
service.schedule > parts.stock
parts.receive > service.schedule
service.repair >> accounting.invoice
service.maintain >> accounting.invoice
marketing.campaign >> sales.show
