# interior design studio
people: Mia, Ethan, Olivia, Noah, Ava
## Consultation | Gathers client requirements and preferences. | why: understanding client needs is essential
a meet: meet with client
t questionnaire: client questionnaire
a listen: discuss client needs
meet >> listen
meet > questionnaire
listen > questionnaire
## Design | Creates sketches and 3D models of the design. | why: visualizing the design is critical
t sketch: design sketch
a model: create 3D models
t plan: floor plan
model > sketch
model > plan
## Sourcing | Finds materials and furniture that match the design. | why: materials need to be sourced to match the design
t catalog: supplier catalog
a search: search for materials
a choose: choose furniture
search > catalog
choose > catalog
## Installation | Oversees the installation of the design.
t schedule: installation schedule
a install: oversee installation
a supervise: supervise contractors
install > schedule
supervise > install
## Review | Finalizes the design and ensures client satisfaction. | why: ensuring client satisfaction is crucial
t feedback: client feedback
a adjust: make adjustments
a finish: finalize design
adjust > feedback
finish > feedback
## cross
consultation.listen > design.model
design.plan > sourcing.catalog
sourcing.choose > installation.install
installation.supervise > review.adjust
review.finish > consultation.meet
