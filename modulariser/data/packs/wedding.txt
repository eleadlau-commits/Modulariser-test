# wedding planning
people: Hannah, Luke, Aunt Rosa, Imran
## Budget | Sets and tracks the money. | why: costs need a limit
t budget: budget spreadsheet
a deposits: pay deposits
deposits > budget
## Guests | Manages the guest list and invitations.
t list: guest list
a invite: send invitations
a rsvp: chase RSVPs
invite > list
invite >> rsvp
rsvp > list
## Venue | Books and arranges the venue.
a visit: visit venues
a bookvenue: book the venue
t layout: seating plan
visit >> bookvenue
## Catering | Picks the menu and caterer.
a tasting: go to the tasting
t menu: wedding menu
a numbers: give final numbers to the caterer
tasting > menu
## Ceremony | Organises the service itself.
t vows: vows
a officiant: book the officiant
t order: order of service
## cross
venue.bookvenue > budget.budget
catering.numbers > guests.list
catering.tasting > budget.budget
venue.layout > guests.list
budget.deposits >> venue.bookvenue
ceremony.officiant > budget.budget
