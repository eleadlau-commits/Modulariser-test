# cinema
people: Sam, Lisa, Mark, Emily, Jordan
## Scheduling | Determines which movies play when and where. | why: movies need to be assigned to screens
t calendar: weekly schedule
a update: update the schedule
a check: check availability
update > calendar
update >> check
## Ticketing | Sells and tracks movie tickets. | why: customers need tickets to enter screenings
t sales: ticket sales
a sell: sell tickets
a print: print tickets
sell >> print
print > sales
## Screening | Prepares and runs movie screenings.
t cuesheet: cue sheet for screenings
a setup: set up the projection
a show: run the screening
setup >> show
show > cuesheet
## Housekeeping | Cleans and maintains the cinema space.
t checklist: daily maintenance checklist
a clean: clean auditoriums and restrooms
a inspect: inspect the premises
clean >> inspect
inspect > checklist
## Marketing | Promotes the cinema and its movies.
t promotion: promotional materials
a advertise: run ads in local media
a social: manage social media
advertise > promotion
social > promotion
## cross
scheduling.update > screening.cuesheet
scheduling.check > housekeeping.inspect
ticketing.print > screening.show
marketing.advertise > ticketing.sales
marketing.social > ticketing.sales
screening.setup > housekeeping.checklist
