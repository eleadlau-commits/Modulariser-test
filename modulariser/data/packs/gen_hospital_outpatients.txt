# hospital outpatients
people: Sarah, Mark, Lisa, David, Amanda
## Scheduling | Manages appointment bookings and cancellations. | why: patients need to be scheduled for appointments
a book: schedule appointments
a cancel: cancel appointments
t calendar: appointment calendar
book > calendar
cancel > calendar
## Reception | Greets patients and checks them in. | why: patients need to be checked in and directed
a greet: greet patients
a checkin: check patients in
t log: check-in log
greet >> checkin
checkin > log
## Medical Consultation | Conducts patient examinations and provides diagnoses. | why: patients need medical attention
t chart: medical chart
a examine: examine patients
a diagnose: diagnose conditions
examine >> diagnose
examine > chart
## Lab Testing | Collects and processes samples for analysis. | why: tests are needed to diagnose
a collect: collect samples
a process: process samples
t results: test results
collect >> process
process > results
## Imaging | Performs imaging studies. | why: imaging can help diagnose conditions
t scan: imaging scan
a perform: perform imaging
a review: review images
perform >> review
review > scan
## Medication | Prescribes and dispenses medication. | why: patients may need medication
a prescribe: prescribe medication
a dispense: dispense medication
t prescription: prescription log
prescribe >> dispense
dispense > prescription
## cross
scheduling.book > reception.greet
medical.examine >> lab.collect
medical.examine >> imaging.perform
medical.diagnose >> medication.prescribe
lab.results > medical.chart
imaging.scan > medical.chart
medication.dispense > medical.chart
