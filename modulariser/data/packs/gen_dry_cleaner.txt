## Collection | Collects clothes and delivers to the store.
a pick: pick up clothes
t bags: collection bags
pick > bags
## Sorting | Sorts clothes by fabric and stain type.
t tag: sorting tags
a sort: sort clothes
t list: sorting list
sort > tag
sort > list
## Cleaning | Cleans clothes using appropriate methods.
t cleaner: cleaning solution
a wash: wash clothes
t machines: cleaning machines
wash > machines
wash > cleaner
## Ironing | Irons clothes to remove wrinkles.
t iron: steam iron
a iron: iron clothes
t board: ironing board
iron > iron
iron > board
## Packaging | Prepares clothes for delivery or pick-up.
a fold: fold clothes
t box: packaging box
a label: label clothes
fold >> label
label > box
