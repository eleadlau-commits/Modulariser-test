# dance school
people: Mia, Ryan, Emma, Leo
## Curriculum | Plans the dance lessons and routines.
t syllabus: dance syllabus
a schedule: schedule classes
t routine: dance routines
schedule > syllabus
schedule > routine
## Instruction | Teaches the dance classes to students.
a teach: teach dance moves
t demo: demonstration video
a correct: correct student's technique
teach > demo
correct > demo
## Performance | Prepares students for public performances.
t calendar: performance calendar
a rehearse: rehearse performance
t costume: costume list
rehearse > costume
rehearse > calendar
## Registration | Manages enrollment and payments for classes.
t roster: class roster
a enroll: enroll new students
t payment: payment records
enroll > roster
enroll > payment
## Evaluation | Assesses student progress and proficiency.
a evaluate: evaluate student performance
t feedback: feedback report
a rank: rank students in levels
evaluate > feedback
rank > feedback
## cross
curriculum.schedule > instruction.teach
curriculum.routine > performance.rehearse
registration.roster > evaluation.evaluate
evaluation.rank > curriculum.syllabus
instruction.correct >> evaluation.rank
performance.calendar > registration.enroll
