# tennis club
people: Alex, Jamie, Mia, Olivia, Ethan
## Membership | Manages the list of members and their payments. | why: to keep track of club members
t list: member list
a register: register new members
t fees: fee schedule
a collect: collect membership fees
register > list
collect > fees
## Scheduling | Plans the use of courts and times for members and guests. | why: to efficiently use the courts
t calendar: court calendar
a book: book court slots
t waitlist: waitlist
book > calendar
book >> waitlist
## Tournaments | Organizes local and external tournaments for members. | why: to provide competitive opportunities
t events: tournament events
a schedule: schedule tournament dates
t entry: entry forms
schedule > events
schedule >> entry
## Coaching | Provides training sessions for members of different skill levels. | why: to improve player skills
t roster: coaching roster
a train: conduct training sessions
t schedule: coaching schedule
train > roster
train > schedule
## Facilities | Maintains the courts, equipment, and club premises. | why: to keep the club in good condition
t maintenance: maintenance log
a check: check courts and equipment
t supplies: supplies inventory
check > maintenance
check >> supplies
## cross
