# trade union branch
people: Sarah, Mark, Linda, Andrew, David
## Membership | Manages the members and their dues.
t roster: member roster
a collect: collect dues
a update: update contact info
collect > roster
update > roster
## Communication | Organizes meetings and sends out updates.
t newsletter: union newsletter
a meeting: organize meetings
a inform: inform members of updates
meeting > newsletter
inform > newsletter
## Advocacy | Represents members' interests to management.
t grievances: list of grievances
a negotiate: negotiate with management
a present: present grievances
negotiate > grievances
present > grievances
## Education | Provides training and materials to members.
t materials: educational materials
a train: conduct training sessions
a distribute: distribute materials
train > materials
distribute > materials
## Legal | Ensures compliance with labor laws and contracts.
t contract: union contract
a review: review legal documents
a consult: consult with legal advisors
review > contract
consult > contract
## cross
membership.collect > advocacy.present
communication.inform > advocacy.present
education.train >> advocacy.present
advocacy.negotiate >> legal.review
advocacy.present >> legal.review
