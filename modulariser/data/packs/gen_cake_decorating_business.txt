# cake decorating business
people: Emma, Lisa, Jack, Sam
## Design | Creates custom designs and templates for cakes. | why: every cake needs a unique design
t template: design template
a sketch: sketch design ideas
t palette: color palette
sketch > template
sketch > palette
## Procurement | Orders and receives ingredients and materials. | why: materials are needed for production
a order: order ingredients and materials
t supplier: supplier list
a receive: receive deliveries
order > supplier
order >> receive
## Production | Assembles and decorates the cakes.
t recipe: production recipe
a bake: bake the cake
a decorate: decorate the cake
bake >> decorate
decorate > recipe
## Quality | Inspects and tests the cakes for quality before delivery.
a inspect: inspect the cake for quality
a test: conduct taste tests
t checklist: quality checklist
inspect > checklist
test > checklist
## Delivery | Prepares and delivers cakes to customers.
a prepare: prepare for delivery
t schedule: delivery schedule
a deliver: deliver the cake
prepare >> deliver
prepare > schedule
## cross
design.template > production.recipe
procurement.order > production.recipe
procurement.receive > quality.checklist
production.decorate >> quality.inspect
quality.inspect >> delivery.prepare
production.decorate > delivery.schedule
