# food truck
people: Sarah, Mike, Lisa, Tom
## Ordering | Manages customer orders and payment. | why: orders must be taken and paid for
a take: take orders
a pay: process payment
t menu: food truck menu
take > menu
take >> pay
## Cooking | Prepares the food according to orders. | why: food must be cooked before serving
t prep: prep list
a cook: cook dishes
t ingredients: ingredient list
cook > prep
cook > ingredients
## Serving | Serves the food to customers. | why: food must be delivered to customers
t orders: served orders
a hand: hand over food
hand > orders
## Cleaning | Cleans tables and dishes to maintain hygiene. | why: hygiene is critical for customer safety
a wash: wash dishes
a clean: clean tables
t supplies: cleaning supplies
wash > supplies
clean > supplies
## Inventory | Tracks inventory of ingredients and supplies. | why: to keep track of what is available and to order more
t stock: inventory stock
a update: update inventory
update > stock
## cross
ordering.take >> cooking.cook
cooking.cook >> serving.hand
serving.hand > inventory.stock
cleaning.wash > inventory.stock
cleaning.clean > inventory.stock
inventory.update > ordering.menu
inventory.update > cooking.ingredients
inventory.update > cleaning.supplies
