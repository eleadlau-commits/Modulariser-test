## Quotation | Estimates the cost and creates a quote. | why: clients need to know the cost before proceeding.
a assess: assess house contents
t estimate: cost estimate
a quote: create quote
assess >> estimate
estimate >> quote
## Scheduling | Plans the move date and logistics. | why: the move needs to be organized.
a plan: plan move date
t calendar: scheduling calendar
t resources: vehicle and manpower resources
plan >> calendar
plan >> resources
## Packing | Prepares items for transport. | why: items need to be protected during transport.
t boxes: packing boxes
a wrap: wrap fragile items
a load: load items into boxes
wrap >> load
load > boxes
## Transport | Moves items from one location to another. | why: items need to be transported.
t vehicle: transport vehicle
a load: load vehicle
a unload: unload vehicle
load >> unload
load > vehicle
## Insurance | Handles insurance claims and policies. | why: items may be damaged during transport.
t policy: insurance policy
a claim: file insurance claims
t coverage: coverage details
claim > policy
claim > coverage
## cross
quotation.assess > packing.wrap
scheduling.plan > transport.load
packing.load > transport.load
transport.unload > insurance.claim
quotation.estimate > insurance.policy
