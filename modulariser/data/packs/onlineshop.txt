# online shop
people: Lily, Arjun, Bo, Nkechi
## Catalogue | Lists products and prices. | why: customers need something to browse
t listings: product listings
a photos: take product photos
a pricing: set prices
photos >> listings
pricing > listings
## Orders | Takes and tracks orders.
a checkout: process checkout
t orderdb: orders table
checkout > orderdb
## Fulfilment | Packs and posts orders.
a packorder: pack orders
a post: post parcels
t tracking: tracking numbers
packorder >> post
post > tracking
## Support | Answers customers and handles returns.
a emails: answer customer emails
a refunds: process refunds
t returns: returns log
refunds > returns
## Marketing | Brings in customers.
a ads: run ads
t discounts: discount codes
ads > discounts
## cross
orders.checkout > catalogue.listings
orders.checkout > marketing.discounts
fulfilment.packorder > orders.orderdb
support.emails > fulfilment.tracking
support.refunds > orders.orderdb
marketing.ads > catalogue.listings
