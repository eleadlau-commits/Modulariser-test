# warehouse fulfilment
people: Dev, Marta, Kofi, Lena
## Inbound | Receives goods from suppliers and puts them away. | why: stock has to arrive
a unload: unload trucks
a putaway: put stock away
t asn: advance shipping notice
unload > asn
unload >> putaway
## Inventory | Knows where everything is and how much.
t wms: inventory system
a cycle: run cycle counts
t bins: bin locations
cycle > wms
## Picking | Picks customer orders off the shelves.
a wave: plan pick waves
a pick: pick items
t picklist: pick list
wave > picklist
pick > picklist
## Packing | Packs and labels parcels.
a pack: pack parcels
a label: print shipping labels
pack >> label
## Dispatch | Hands parcels to carriers.
t manifest: carrier manifest
a handover: hand over to the carrier
handover > manifest
## cross
inbound.putaway > inventory.bins
inbound.putaway > inventory.wms
picking.wave > inventory.wms
picking.pick > inventory.bins
picking.pick >> packing.pack
packing.label >> dispatch.handover
dispatch.manifest > packing.label
