# optician
people: Lisa, Mark, Sarah, David, Emily
## Consultation | Examines patient's vision and discusses options.
t records: patient records
a examine: conduct eye examination
a discuss: discuss options with patient
examine > records
examine >> discuss
## Prescription | Writes and explains the prescription to the patient.
t prescription: written prescription
a explain: explain prescription details
a confirm: confirm patient understanding
explain > prescription
explain >> confirm
## Dispensing | Measures and fits the patient's new glasses.
t glasses: new glasses
a measure: take measurements
a fit: fit glasses to patient
measure >> fit
measure > glasses
## Repair | Fixes broken or damaged glasses.
t repairs: repair log
a fix: fix broken glasses
a log: log repair work
fix >> log
fix > repairs
## Billing | Handles payment and insurance claims for services.
t invoice: service invoice
a charge: charge patient or insurance
a settle: settle payment
charge >> settle
settle > invoice
## cross
consultation.examine > prescription.explain
dispensing.measure > prescription.prescription
repair.fix > dispensing.glasses
billing.settle > consultation.records
billing.charge > repair.repairs
billing.charge > dispensing.glasses
