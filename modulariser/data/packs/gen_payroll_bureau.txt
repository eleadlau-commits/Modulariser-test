# payroll bureau
people: Jane, Mark, Lisa, Tom, Emily
## Collection | Receives and verifies employee timesheets and pay details.
a receive: receive timesheets
t validation: validation checklist
a verify: verify timesheet accuracy
receive >> validation
receive >> verify
## Calculation | Computes gross and net pay based on rules.
t rules: pay rules document
a compute: calculate gross pay
a adjust: apply tax and deductions
compute >> adjust
## Processing | Handles the payroll transactions and schedules payments.
t transactions: payroll transactions
a process: process payroll transactions
a schedule: schedule bank transfers
process >> transactions
process >> schedule
## Reporting | Generates and distributes payroll reports.
t report: payroll report
a generate: generate payroll report
a distribute: distribute report to managers
generate >> report
generate >> distribute
## Compliance | Ensures payroll complies with legal standards.
t regulations: legal regulations
a audit: audit payroll for compliance
a update: update compliance rules
audit > regulations
update > regulations
## cross
collection.receive > calculation.rules
calculation.adjust > processing.transactions
processing.schedule > reporting.distribute
compliance.audit > processing.transactions
compliance.update > calculation.rules
