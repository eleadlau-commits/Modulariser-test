# vineyard tasting room
people: Emma, Chris, Olivia, Jake
## Reception | Manages guest check-in and initial experience.
t register: guest register
a welcome: welcome guests
a direct: direct to tasting area
welcome >> direct
welcome > register
## Tasting | Conducts wine tastings and educates guests.
t menu: tasting menu
a pour: pour wine samples
a explain: explain wine notes
pour >> explain
pour > menu
## Sales | Handles wine purchases and customer orders.
t inventory: wine inventory list
a sell: sell wines to guests
a package: package wine orders
sell >> package
sell > inventory
## Tours | Organizes and leads vineyard tours.
t itinerary: tour itinerary
a lead: lead vineyard tours
a answer: answer questions during tours
lead >> answer
lead > itinerary
## Billing | Manages payment processing and receipts.
t receipt: sales receipt
a process: process payment
a give: give receipt to guest
process >> give
process > receipt
## cross
reception.direct >> tasting.pour
tasting.explain >> sales.sell
sales.package >> billing.process
tours.answer >> reception.register
tours.lead >> tasting.pour
billing.process > sales.inventory
