assess > casefile
assess > laws
## Documentation | Prepares all necessary documents and forms for the application. | why: required to submit a complete and accurate application
a draft: draft application documents
t checklist: document checklist

draft >> review
review > checklist
## Communication | Manages all client interactions and updates. | why: crucial for maintaining trust and keeping the client informed
a consult: conduct consultations with clients
t calendar: schedule calendar
a update: provide updates to clients
consult > calendar
consult >> update
## Submission | Submits the application to the relevant authorities. | why: the final step to initiate the legal process
a submit: submit the application
t proof: proof of submission
submit > proof
