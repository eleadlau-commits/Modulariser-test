# coffee roastery
people: Sam, Lucy, Alex, Mia, Ethan
## Sourcing | Finds and selects green coffee beans. | why: the quality of the coffee depends on the quality of the beans
a scout: scout for new suppliers
t profiles: supplier profiles
a taste: taste test samples
scout > profiles
scout >> taste
## Roasting | Roasts the green coffee beans into ready-to-brew coffee. | why: roasting transforms the green beans into a flavor-rich product
t batches: roast batches
a roast: roast beans
a cool: cool the roasted beans
roast >> cool
cool > batches
## Quality Control | Tests the roasted coffee to ensure it meets standards. | why: quality control ensures product consistency
a cup: cupping evaluation
a measure: measure roast profiles
t standards: quality standards
cup > standards
measure > standards
## Packaging | Prepares the roasted coffee for distribution. | why: packaging preserves the freshness and quality of the coffee
a package: package the coffee
t labels: product labels
a seal: seal the packaging
package > labels
package >> seal
## Sales | Connects with customers and manages orders. | why: sales are how the roastery generates revenue
t orders: sales orders
a quote: provide quotes
a ship: ship orders
quote > orders
ship > orders
## cross
sourcing.taste > quality.cup
roasting.roast > quality.measure
roasting.batches > packaging.package
sales.quote > sourcing.profiles
sales.orders >> packaging.seal
