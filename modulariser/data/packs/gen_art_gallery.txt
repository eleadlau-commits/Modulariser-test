# art gallery
people: Emily, Leo, Olivia, Sam, Ava
## Curation | Selects and organizes artworks for exhibitions.
t collection: artwork collection
a choose: choose artworks
t theme: exhibition theme
choose > collection
choose > theme
## Conservation | Ensures artworks are preserved properly.
t records: conservation records
a inspect: inspect artworks
a document: document condition
inspect >> document
document > records
## Exhibition | Sets up and manages the exhibition space.
t layout: exhibition layout
a install: install artworks
t lighting: lighting setup
install > layout
install > lighting
## Marketing | Promotes the gallery and its exhibitions.
t brochure: exhibition brochure
a advertise: advertise exhibition
t social: social media posts
advertise > brochure
advertise > social
## Sales | Handles the buying and selling of artworks.
a sell: sell artworks
t inventory: inventory list
a appraise: appraise artwork value
sell > inventory
appraise >> sell
## Events | Organizes public events and artist talks.
t schedule: event schedule
a invite: invite artists
a host: host events
invite >> schedule
host > schedule
## cross
curation.choose > conservation.inspect
exhibition.install > conservation.records
marketing.advertise > sales.inventory
events.invite > curation.theme
events.host >> exhibition.install
sales.appraise > curation.collection
exhibition.layout > events.schedule
