# airport check-in
people: Gita, Mark, Ines, Tobi
## Check-in | Checks passengers onto the flight. | why: passengers need boarding passes
a checkin: check passengers in
t manifest: passenger manifest
a passes: issue boarding passes
checkin > manifest
checkin >> passes
## Baggage | Tags and loads bags.
a tag: tag bags
a load: load the hold
t bagcount: bag reconciliation
tag >> load
load > bagcount
## Security | Screens passengers.
a screen: screen passengers
t watchlist: watch list
screen > watchlist
## Boarding | Boards the aircraft.
a board: board passengers
t gate: gate list
board > gate
## Loadcontrol | Balances the aircraft.
a loadsheet: prepare the load sheet
t weights: weight figures
loadsheet > weights
## cross
boarding.gate > checkin.manifest
loadcontrol.loadsheet > baggage.bagcount
loadcontrol.loadsheet > checkin.manifest
checkin.passes >> security.screen
security.screen >> boarding.board
baggage.bagcount > checkin.manifest
