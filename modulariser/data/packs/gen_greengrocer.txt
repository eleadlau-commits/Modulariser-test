# greengrocer
people: Sarah, Michael, Emily, David, Lisa
## Sourcing | Procures fresh produce from various suppliers. | why: produce needs to be sourced to stock the store
a order: place orders with suppliers
t suppliers: list of suppliers
a inspect: inspect deliveries
order > suppliers
order >> inspect
## Inventory | Manages the stock levels and freshness of produce. 
t sheet: inventory sheet
a check: check stock levels
a rotate: rotate stock to ensure freshness
check > sheet
rotate > sheet
## Display | Arranges produce in an appealing manner for customers.
t layout: display layout
a arrange: arrange produce on display
a restock: restock depleted sections
arrange > layout
restock > layout
## Sales | Handles customer interactions and transactions.
t ledger: sales ledger
a serve: serve customers
a process: process payments
serve >> process
process > ledger
## Cleaning | Ensures the store and produce areas are clean and organized.
a clean: clean display areas
a organize: organize storage areas
clean >> organize
## cross
sourcing.inspect > inventory.sheet
inventory.check > display.layout
inventory.rotate > display.layout
sales.serve > display.layout
cleaning.clean > display.layout
