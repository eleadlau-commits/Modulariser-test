# athletics club
people: Alex, Mia, Ben, Carla
## Membership | Manages membership and registration.
t roster: member roster
a register: register new members
a update: update member information
register > roster
update > roster
## Training | Plans and conducts training sessions.
t schedule: training schedule
a train: conduct training sessions
a plan: plan training sessions
plan >> train
train > schedule
## Competitions | Prepares for and participates in competitions.
t calendar: competition calendar
a prepare: prepare for competitions
a compete: compete in events
prepare >> compete
prepare > calendar
## Health | Tracks injuries and ensures medical readiness.
t log: injury log
a check: check medical readiness
a treat: treat injuries
check > log
treat > log
## Events | Organizes and runs club events.
t plan: event plan
a organize: organize events
a run: run events
organize >> run
organize > plan
## cross
membership.register > training.schedule
training.train > health.check
competitions.prepare >> training.train
health.treat >> training.train
events.organize >> membership.update
events.run >> competitions.prepare
