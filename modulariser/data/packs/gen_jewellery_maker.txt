# jewellery maker
people: Emma, Leo, Grace, Noah
## Design | Creates sketches and selects materials.
t sketch: design sketch
a choose: choose materials
t material: material list
choose >> sketch
sketch > material
## Crafting | Assembles the jewellery piece.
a assemble: assemble components
t component: component list
a polish: polish piece
assemble >> polish
assemble > component
## Finishing | Adds final touches and packaging.
a package: package jewellery
t box: jewellery box
a label: label packaging
package >> label
package > box
## Sales | Manages orders and customer relations.
t order: order form
a contact: contact customers
t invoice: invoice
contact >> invoice
contact > order
## Inventory | Tracks materials and finished pieces.
t ledger: inventory ledger
a update: update stock levels
t stock: stock list
update > ledger
update > stock
## cross
design.material > inventory.stock
crafting.component > inventory.stock
finishing.box > inventory.stock
sales.order > inventory.stock
design.sketch >> crafting.assemble
crafting.polish >> finishing.package
finishing.package > sales.invoice
inventory.update > sales.contact
inventory.update > design.material
