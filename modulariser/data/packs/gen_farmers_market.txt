# farmers market
people: Sarah, John, Emily, Michael
## Setup | Prepares the market space and structures for vendors. | why: vendors need a clear and organized space to operate
t layout: market layout plan
a setup: setup stalls and structures
t signage: market signage
setup > layout
setup > signage
## Vendor Management | Registers vendors and checks their inventory. | why: ensures a variety of goods and fair play
t registry: vendor registry
a register: register vendors
a check: check inventory
register > registry
check > registry
## Logistics | Handles the transport of goods to the market. | why: goods must arrive fresh and in good condition
t schedule: delivery schedule
a transport: transport goods to the market
a unload: unload goods at the stalls
transport > schedule
transport >> unload
## Sales | Manages the selling process and customer interactions. | why: ensures smooth transactions and customer satisfaction
t payment: payment processing
a sell: sell goods to customers
a assist: assist customers with purchases
sell > payment
assist > sell
## Cleanup | Cleans up the market space after the event. | why: keeps the market space tidy and attractive for future events
t waste: waste management plan
a clean: clean stalls and structures
a pack: pack up equipment
clean > waste
clean >> pack
## cross
setup.layout > vendor.registry
logistics.unload > sales.sell
sales.payment > cleanup.pack
vendor.check > logistics.schedule
logistics.transport >> vendor.register
sales.sell >> cleanup.clean
