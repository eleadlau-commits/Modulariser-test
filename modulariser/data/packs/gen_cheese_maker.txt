# cheese maker
people: Ana, Raj, Mei, Tom
## Milk Sourcing | Obtains the milk for cheese production. | why: milk is the primary ingredient
t suppliers: supplier list
a inspect: inspect milk quality
a order: order milk deliveries
inspect > suppliers
order >> inspect
## Curdling | Curdles the milk and adds starter cultures. | why: curdling is essential for cheese formation
t rennet: rennet
a add: add starter cultures
a curdle: curdle the milk
add >> curdle
curdle > rennet
## Draining | Drains the curds to remove whey. | why: draining is necessary for cheese texture
a drain: drain the curds
a press: press the curds
drain >> press
## Aging | Ages the cheese to develop flavor. | why: aging is crucial for taste
t cave: aging cave
a age: age the cheese
age > cave
## Packaging | Prepares the cheese for sale. | why: packaging is essential for sales
a wrap: wrap the cheese
t label: cheese label
wrap >> label
## cross
milk.order > curdling.add
draining.press >> aging.age
aging.age > packaging.wrap
packaging.label > milk.suppliers
