# window cleaning round
people: Sarah, Jack, Lisa, Mike, Emily
## Scheduling | Coordinates the schedule for the round. | why: ensures timely organization of tasks
t schedule: weekly schedule
a assign: assign tasks to cleaners
a update: update availability
assign > schedule
update > schedule
## Preparation | Prepares the necessary equipment and materials. | why: ensures the needed tools are ready
t kit: cleaning kit
a check: check equipment
a prepare: prepare cleaning supplies
check > kit
prepare > kit
## Cleaning | Cleans the windows of assigned properties.
t list: property list
a access: access properties
a clean: clean windows
access > list
clean > access
## Inspection | Inspects the cleaned windows for quality assurance.
t checklist: quality checklist
a inspect: inspect windows
a report: report issues
inspect > checklist
report > checklist
## Reporting | Generates the final report of the cleaning round.
t report: cleaning report
a summarize: summarize the round
a review: review inspection reports
summarize > report
review > report
## cross
scheduling.assign > cleaning.list
preparation.check > cleaning.access
preparation.prepare > cleaning.clean
cleaning.clean >> inspection.inspect
inspection.report >> reporting.review
reporting.summarize > scheduling.update
