# yoga studio
people: Priya, Ben, Alex, Mei
## Scheduling | Manages class schedules and instructor assignments. | why: classes need to be planned and instructors need to know their duties.
t calendar: class schedule
a assign: assign instructors to classes
a update: update calendar as needed
assign > calendar
update > calendar
## Marketing | Promotes the studio and attracts new customers.
t flyer: promotional flyer
a post: post on social media
a email: send email newsletters
post > flyer
email > flyer
## Registration | Handles customer registration and payment for classes.
t register: customer registration form
a collect: collect payment
a confirm: confirm registration
collect > register
confirm > register
## Instruction | Teaches yoga classes and provides individual guidance to customers.
t guide: teaching guide
a teach: teach yoga classes
a assist: assist individual customers
teach > guide
assist > guide
## Cleaning | Ensures the studio is clean and safe for use.
t checklist: cleaning checklist
a clean: clean studio after classes
a restock: restock supplies
clean > checklist
restock > checklist
## cross
scheduling.assign > instruction.teach
marketing.post >> registration.collect
registration.confirm >> instruction.teach
cleaning.clean >> scheduling.calendar
cleaning.restock >> instruction.teach
