## Planning | Coordinates the display logistics and design. | why: a plan is needed for any display
t schedule: display schedule
a design: design fireworks sequences
t permits: required permits
design > schedule
permits >> schedule
## Procurement | Orders fireworks and safety equipment. | why: materials need to be acquired
a order: order fireworks
t equipment: safety equipment
a check: check deliveries
order > equipment
check > order
## Setup | Sets up the launch sites and safety perimeters. | why: setup is critical for safety and performance
t sites: launch sites
a layout: layout the sites
t perimeters: safety perimeters
layout > sites
layout > perimeters
## Control | Manages the firing sequence and timing. | why: timing is crucial for the display
t sequence: firing sequence
a fire: fire the fireworks
a time: time the cues
fire > sequence
time >> fire
## Safety | Ensures that all safety protocols are followed. | why: safety protocols must be followed
a inspect: inspect equipment
a monitor: monitor the perimeter
t protocols: safety protocols
inspect > protocols
monitor > protocols
## Cleanup | Cleans up the site and disposes of debris. | why: cleanup is necessary after the display
a collect: collect debris
a dispose: dispose of debris
a clear: clear the site
collect >> dispose
dispose >> clear
