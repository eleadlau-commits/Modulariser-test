# pet shop
people: Bella, Tom, Max, Lucy, Jake
## Purchasing | Orders and receives pet supplies and animals. | why: inventory needs to be replenished
a order: order stock
t suppliers: list of suppliers
a receive: receive deliveries
order > suppliers
order >> receive
## Inventory | Tracks and manages the store's stock levels.
t sheet: inventory sheet
a count: count stock weekly
count > sheet
## Care | Provides daily care and attention to animals.
t list: care checklist
a feed: feed animals
a clean: clean cages
feed > list
clean > list
## Sales | Handles customer interactions and sales transactions.
t register: sales register
a assist: assist customers
a sell: sell pets and supplies
assist >> sell
sell > register
## Grooming | Offers grooming services for pets.
t schedule: grooming schedule
a groom: groom pets
a wash: wash pets
groom > schedule
wash > schedule
## cross
purchasing.receive > inventory.sheet
care.clean > inventory.sheet
sales.sell > inventory.sheet
grooming.schedule >> sales.assist
inventory.sheet > sales.assist
purchasing.order > grooming.schedule
