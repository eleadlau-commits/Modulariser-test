# notary office
people: Anna, Brian, Carlos, Daisy, Ethan
## Document Preparation | Prepares and reviews documents for notarization.
t document: document drafts
a review: review document content
a edit: edit document for accuracy
review > document
edit > document
## Client Consultation | Advises clients on notarial requirements and procedures.
t form: client information form
a advise: advise on notarial requirements
a interview: interview clients
interview > form
advise > form
## Notarization | Verifies identity and witnesses signatures.
t id: client identification
a verify: verify client identity
a witness: witness signatures
verify > id
verify >> witness
## Record Keeping | Maintains records of notarized documents.
t record: notary records
a log: log notarizations
a store: store notarized documents
log > record
store > record
## Fees | Collects and processes payments for notarial services.
a charge: charge for notarial services
t receipt: payment receipt
a collect: collect payment
charge >> collect
collect > receipt
## cross
document.edit > notarization.verify
client.interview > document.document
notarization.witness > record.log
record.log > fees.charge
fees.collect > record.store
