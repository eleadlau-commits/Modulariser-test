# car dealership
people: Jim, Sarah, Mike, Lisa, Tom
## Sales | Sells cars to customers.
t inventory: car inventory list
a show: show cars to customers
a deal: negotiate deals
show > inventory
show >> deal
## Finance | Handles the financing and payment process.
a credit: check credit
a payment: process payments
a finance: arrange financing
credit >> payment
credit >> finance
## Service | Maintains and repairs cars.
t schedule: service schedule
a repair: perform repairs
a maintain: perform maintenance
repair > schedule
maintain > schedule
## Parts | Orders and stocks parts for repairs.
t stock: parts stock
a order: order parts
a receive: receive parts delivery
order > stock
receive > stock
## Body Shop | Repairs and paints car bodies.
t supplies: supplies
a repair: repair body
a paint: paint car
repair > supplies
paint > supplies
## cross
sales.show > service.schedule
sales.deal > finance.credit
finance.payment > parts.order
parts.receive > service.repair
parts.receive > body.repair
service.maintain > parts.stock
body.paint > service.schedule
