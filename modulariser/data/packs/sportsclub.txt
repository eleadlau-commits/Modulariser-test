# amateur football club
people: Coach Dave, Mel, Tunde, Rosie
## Membership | Signs up players and collects subs. | why: players must be registered
t register: player register
a subs: collect subs
a signup: sign up new players
subs > register
signup > register
## Coaching | Runs training and picks teams.
a training: run training sessions
a selection: pick the team
t plan: training plan
training > plan
## Fixtures | Arranges matches.
t calendar: fixture list
a referee: book referees
a pitch: book the pitch
referee > calendar
pitch > calendar
## Kit | Buys and washes kit.
t kitlist: kit inventory
a wash: wash the kit
wash > kitlist
## Treasury | Keeps the accounts.
t accounts: club accounts
a fees: pay league fees
fees > accounts
## cross
coaching.selection > membership.register
coaching.selection > fixtures.calendar
membership.subs > treasury.accounts
fixtures.pitch > treasury.accounts
kit.kitlist > membership.register
fixtures.referee > treasury.accounts
