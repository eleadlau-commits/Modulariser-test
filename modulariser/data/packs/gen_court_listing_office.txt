# court listing office
people: Sarah, John, Maria, David, Emily
## Case Intake | Receives new cases and assigns them to a category. | why: cases must be categorized to be listed appropriately
a intake: receive new cases
t categories: case categories
a assign: assign cases to categories
intake >> assign
assign > categories
## Calendar Management | Schedules court dates. | why: court dates must be scheduled to avoid conflicts
t calendar: court calendar
a schedule: schedule court dates
a conflict: check for date conflicts
schedule >> conflict
conflict > calendar
## Notification | Notifies parties involved in the cases. | why: parties need to be informed of their court dates
a notify: send notifications
t contact: contact information
notify > contact
## Record Keeping | Maintains records of all court cases. | why: records are necessary for legal and administrative purposes
t files: case files
a update: update case files
update > files
## Compliance | Ensures all court procedures comply with legal standards. | why: compliance with legal standards is mandatory
a review: review compliance
t regulations: legal regulations
review > regulations
## cross
case.intake > calendar.calendar
case.assign > record.files
calendar.schedule > notification.notify
notification.contact > record.files
record.update > compliance.regulations
compliance.review > case.categories
