# shipping container depot
people: Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave, Erin
## Reception | Checks in incoming containers and updates tracking. | why: containers need to be tracked and managed
a receive: receive containers
t manifest: incoming manifest
a update: update tracking system
receive > manifest
receive >> update
## Storage | Assigns containers to storage locations and moves them. | why: containers need to be stored efficiently
t locations: storage locations
a assign: assign containers to locations
a move: move containers to locations
assign > locations
move > locations
## Inspection | Inspects containers for damage or discrepancies. | why: ensures containers are not damaged before further handling
a inspect: inspect containers
t report: inspection report
inspect > report
## Loading | Loads containers onto outgoing vehicles and updates tracking. | why: containers need to be loaded for transport
a load: load containers onto vehicles
t vehicle: outgoing vehicle
a update: update tracking system
load > vehicle
load >> update
## Maintenance | Maintains and repairs container handling equipment. | why: ensures equipment is in good working order
a inspect: inspect equipment
t repair: repair logs
a repair: perform repairs
inspect > repair
repair > repair
## cross
reception.receive > storage.assign
storage.assign > inspection.inspect
inspection.inspect > loading.load
loading.update > reception.update
maintenance.inspect > reception.receive
maintenance.repair > storage.move
maintenance.repair > loading.load
