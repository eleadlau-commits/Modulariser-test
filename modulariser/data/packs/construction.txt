## Planning | Plans the programme of works. | why: work needs a sequence
t programme: programme of works
a lookahead: write the two-week lookahead
lookahead > programme
## Procurement | Buys materials and hires plant.
a po: raise purchase orders
a hire: hire plant
t deliveries: delivery schedule
po > deliveries
## Safety | Keeps the site safe.
a induct: induct workers
t rams: method statements
a inspect: do safety inspections
induct > rams
## Groundworks | Digs and pours foundations.
a dig: dig foundations
a pour: pour concrete
dig >> pour
## Quality | Inspects finished work.
t snags: snag list
a signoff: sign off work
signoff > snags
## cross
procurement.deliveries > planning.programme
groundworks.dig > safety.rams
groundworks.pour > procurement.deliveries
quality.signoff > planning.programme
groundworks.pour >> quality.signoff
safety.induct >> groundworks.dig
