# poultry farm
people: Mike, Sarah, John, Lisa
## Breeding | Manages the breeding process to ensure a steady supply of chicks.
t brooders: brooder facilities
a hatch: hatch eggs
a select: select chicks for breeding
hatch > brooders
hatch >> select
## Feeding | Provides nutrition to the flock to ensure health and productivity.
a mix: mix feed
a distribute: distribute feed
a monitor: monitor consumption
mix >> distribute
distribute >> monitor
## Health | Monitors and maintains the health of the flock.
t vaccines: vaccine schedule
a vaccinate: administer vaccines
a inspect: inspect flock for signs of illness
vaccinate > vaccines
vaccinate >> inspect
## Housing | Maintains living conditions for the flock.
t coop: coop structure
a clean: clean coops
a maintain: maintain coop structure
clean > coop
maintain > coop
## Harvesting | Collects eggs and prepares birds for market.
t crates: crates for eggs/birds
a collect: collect eggs/birds
a prepare: prepare for market
collect >> prepare
collect > crates
## cross
breeding.hatch > feeding.distribute
health.vaccinate >> housing.clean
housing.clean > feeding.monitor
harvesting.collect > feeding.distribute
harvesting.prepare >> health.inspect
