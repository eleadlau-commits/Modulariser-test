# arable farm
people: Emily, Jake, Sarah, Mike, Linda
## Planning | Determines what crops to plant and when. | why: ensures crop rotation and profitability
t calendar: planting calendar
a analyze: analyze soil quality
t market: market demands
analyze > calendar
market > calendar
## Cultivation | Prepares the soil and plants the crops.
a plow: plow the fields
a plant: plant seeds
t equipment: farming equipment
plow >> plant
plant > equipment
## Irrigation | Provides water to the crops as needed.
a water: water the fields
t system: irrigation system
a monitor: monitor water levels
water > system
monitor > system
## Harvest | Collects the crops at the end of the growing season.
a harvest: harvest crops
t storage: storage facilities
a transport: transport to market
harvest >> transport
harvest > storage
## Maintenance | Keeps the farm's infrastructure in good condition.
a repair: repair equipment
t tools: maintenance tools
a inspect: inspect facilities
repair > tools
inspect > tools
## cross
planning.calendar > cultivation.plant
cultivation.plant > irrigation.water
irrigation.water >> harvest.harvest
harvest.storage > maintenance.inspect
cultivation.equipment > maintenance.tools
