## Payables | Pays suppliers. | why: bills need paying
a enter: enter supplier invoices
a payrun: run the payment run
t aplist: aged creditors
enter >> payrun
payrun > aplist
## Receivables | Bills customers and chases debt.
a raise: raise sales invoices
a chase: chase overdue invoices
t arlist: aged debtors
chase > arlist
## Payroll | Pays staff.
a payroll: run payroll
t timesheets: timesheets
payroll > timesheets
## Ledger | Keeps the books balanced.
t gl: general ledger
a reconcile: reconcile the bank
a journals: post accruals
reconcile > gl
journals > gl
## Reporting | Produces the management accounts.
a pack: build the management pack
t variance: budget variance report
pack > variance
## cross
payables.payrun > ledger.gl
