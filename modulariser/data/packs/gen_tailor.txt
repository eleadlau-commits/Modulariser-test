# tailor
people: Emily, David, Maria, Liam
## Consultation | Meets with the client to understand their needs. | why: tailoring requires personalized input
a meet: meet with client
t style: style preferences
a measure: take measurements
meet > style
meet >> measure
## Design | Creates the design based on the consultation. | why: every item needs a design
t sketch: design sketch
a customize: customize based on client needs
a select: select fabric
customize > sketch
select > sketch
## Cutting | Prepares the fabric for assembly. | why: fabric needs to be cut according to the design
t pieces: fabric pieces
a cut: cut fabric
a mark: mark on fabric
cut > pieces
mark > pieces
## Assembly | Puts the pieces together into the final garment. | why: this is the core of tailoring
t garment: assembled garment
a sew: sew garment
a pin: pin pieces together
pin > garment
sew > garment
## Finishing | Adds the final touches to the garment. | why: ensures the garment looks perfect
t finished: finished garment
a press: press garment
a detail: add final details
press > finished
detail > finished
## Delivery | Prepares for and delivers the completed garment to the client. | why: the client needs to receive the garment
a pack: pack the garment
t receipt: delivery receipt
a present: present to client
pack >> present
present > receipt
## cross
consultation.style > design.customize
consultation.measure > cutting.cut
design.sketch > cutting.mark
design.select > cutting.cut
assembly.sew >> finishing.press
assembly.pin >> finishing.detail
finishing.finished > delivery.pack
delivery.present > consultation.meet
