# ambulance dispatch
people: Mike, Sarah, John, Lisa, Tom
## Dispatch | Assigns ambulances to calls based on severity and location.
t calls: call log
a assign: assign ambulance to call
t availability: vehicle availability
assign > calls
assign > availability
## Triaging | Prioritizes calls based on symptom severity.
t symptoms: symptom checklist
a assess: assess severity
a prioritize: prioritize calls
assess > symptoms
assess >> prioritize
## Communication | Contacts paramedics and provides call details.
t instructions: call instructions
a contact: contact assigned ambulance
a provide: provide call details
contact > instructions
provide > instructions
## Logistics | Tracks ambulance locations and ensures fuel and supplies.
t tracking: GPS tracking
t supplies: supply inventory
a check: check fuel and supplies
check > supplies
check > tracking
## Reporting | Logs the outcomes of calls and provides feedback.
t outcomes: call outcomes
a log: log call outcome
a feedback: provide feedback
log > outcomes
feedback > outcomes
## cross
dispatch.calls > triaging.assess
dispatch.assign > communication.contact
triaging.prioritize > dispatch.assign
logistics.check > dispatch.availability
reporting.log > logistics.tracking
reporting.feedback > dispatch.calls
