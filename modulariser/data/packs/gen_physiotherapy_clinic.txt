# physiotherapy clinic
people: Emily, David, Lisa, Sam, Olivia
## Assessment | Determines the patient's condition and needs.
a examine: perform initial examination
t history: patient medical history
a diagnose: diagnose the condition
examine > history
examine >> diagnose
## Plan | Creates a customized treatment plan.
t goals: treatment goals
a customize: customize plan based on assessment
a schedule: schedule treatment sessions
customize > goals
customize >> schedule
## Treatment | Conducts physiotherapy sessions.
a apply: apply physiotherapy techniques
t equipment: physiotherapy equipment
a monitor: monitor patient progress
apply > equipment
apply >> monitor
## Review | Evaluates the progress and modifies the plan if necessary.
a assess: assess patient improvement
t notes: progress notes
a modify: modify treatment plan
assess > notes
assess >> modify
## Documentation | Records all patient interactions and progress.
t file: patient file
a update: update patient records
a report: report to referring physician
update > file
report > file
## cross
assessment.examine > plan.customize
assessment.diagnose > plan.customize
treatment.apply > review.assess
treatment.monitor > review.assess
plan.schedule > treatment.apply
documentation.update > review.assess
review.modify > treatment.apply
