# pizza delivery shop
people: Maria, John, Luis, Emily, Sam
## Ordering | Takes and processes customer orders. | why: orders need to be taken to start the process.
a receive: receive customer orders
t menu: pizza menu
a confirm: confirm order details
receive >> confirm
confirm > menu
## Preparation | Assembles and cooks the pizzas. | why: pizzas have to be prepared before delivery.
t toppings: available toppings
a assemble: assemble pizzas
a cook: cook pizzas
assemble > toppings
assemble >> cook
## Packaging | Packages the pizzas for delivery. | why: pizzas need to be properly packaged for delivery.
t boxes: pizza boxes
a pack: package pizzas
a seal: seal packages
pack > boxes
pack >> seal
## Dispatch | Manages delivery logistics. | why: someone needs to organize the deliveries.
t deliveries: delivery list
a assign: assign deliveries to drivers
a route: plan delivery routes
assign > deliveries
route >> assign
## Tracking | Tracks orders and updates customer status. | why: customers need to know the status of their orders.
t tracking: tracking system
a update: update order status
a notify: notify customers of status
update > tracking
update >> notify
## cross
ordering.confirm > preparation.assemble
preparation.cook > packaging.pack
packaging.seal > dispatch.assign
dispatch.assign >> tracking.update
tracking.notify > ordering.receive
