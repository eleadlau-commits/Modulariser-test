# medical device manufacturer
people: Elena, Rafael, Lisa, Andrew, Carlos
## Design | Creates the specifications and design of the medical device. | why: defines the technical requirements and design
a draft: draft initial design
t specs: technical specifications
a refine: refine design based on feedback
draft >> specs
refine > specs
## Testing | Ensures the device meets safety and performance standards. | why: critical for regulatory approval and safety
a validate: perform validation tests
t protocol: test protocol
a document: document test results
validate > protocol
validate >> document
## Manufacturing | Produces the device according to the design and specifications.
t assembly: assembly instructions
a assemble: assemble device
t inspection: quality inspection report
assemble > assembly
assemble >> inspection
## Quality Control | Ensures the manufacturing process meets the standards.
a inspect: perform quality inspections
t report: quality control report
a correct: correct any issues found
inspect >> report
correct > report
## Regulatory | Handles all regulatory filings and approvals.
t submission: regulatory submission
a submit: submit for approval
t compliance: compliance documentation
submit > submission
compliance > submission
## Marketing | Prepares the product for market introduction.
t brochure: marketing brochure
a launch: plan product launch
t campaign: marketing campaign plan
launch > brochure
launch > campaign
## cross
design.specs > manufacturing.assembly
design.specs > quality.inspect
testing.protocol > manufacturing.inspection
testing.document > quality.correct
manufacturing.assembly > quality.inspect
quality.report > manufacturing.inspection
regulatory.compliance > testing.protocol
regulatory.submit > marketing.brochure
marketing.launch > regulatory.compliance
