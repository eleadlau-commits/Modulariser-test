# geological survey team
people: Alex, Jamie, Casey, Sam, Taylor
## Field Survey | Collects geological data and samples in the field.
a locate: locate sampling sites
t samples: geological samples
a record: record field data
locate >> record
record > samples
## Sample Analysis | Analyzes collected samples to identify minerals and structures.
a prepare: prepare samples for analysis
t microscope: microscopes
a analyze: analyze samples
prepare >> analyze
analyze > microscope
## Data Processing | Processes field data for analysis and mapping.
a import: import field data
t software: data processing software
a process: process and clean data
import >> process
process > software
## Mapping | Creates geological maps and models based on data.
t maps: geological maps
a create: create maps
a model: create 3D models
create > maps
model > maps
## Reporting | Compiles and presents findings for stakeholders.
a compile: compile survey findings
t report: final report
a present: present findings
compile > report
present > report
## cross
field.locate > mapping.create
field.record > data.import
sample.analyze > data.import
data.process >> mapping.create
mapping.create >> reporting.compile
mapping.model >> reporting.compile
