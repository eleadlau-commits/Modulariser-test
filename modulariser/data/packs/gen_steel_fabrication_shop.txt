# steel fabrication shop
people: Jack, Lisa, Mike, Sarah, Tom
## Design | Creates the detailed blueprints for fabrication.
t blueprint: detailed blueprint
a model: model parts in CAD
a dimension: add dimensions
model >> dimension
model > blueprint
dimension > blueprint
## Cutting | Cuts the steel to the required dimensions.
t raw: raw steel stock
a cut: cut to dimensions
a measure: measure cuts
cut > measure
cut > raw
## Forming | Shapes the cut steel into the required form.
t shape: shaped parts
a bend: bend steel
a weld: weld parts together
bend >> weld
bend > shape
weld > shape
## Finishing | Prepares the fabricated parts for final inspection.
t finish: finished parts
a polish: polish surfaces
a inspect: final inspection
polish >> inspect
polish > finish
inspect > finish
## Assembly | Assembles the finished parts into the final product.
t product: assembled product
a fit: fit parts together
a check: check for alignment
fit >> check
fit > product
check > product
## cross
design.blueprint > cutting.raw
design.blueprint > forming.shape
forming.weld > finishing.finish
forming.shape > finishing.finish
finishing.finish > assembly.fit
assembly.product > cutting.raw
