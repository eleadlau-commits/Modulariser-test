# freight forwarder
people: Alex, Jamie, Raj, Priya
## Booking | Coordinates shipments with clients and carriers.
t booking: booking form
a confirm: confirm shipping details
a schedule: schedule pickup
confirm >> schedule
## Pricing | Calculates and negotiates costs for clients.
t pricelist: pricing list
a quote: provide a quote
a adjust: adjust pricing based on negotiation
quote > pricelist
quote >> adjust
## Documentation | Prepares and files necessary documents.
t documents: shipping documents
a create: create shipping documents
a file: file shipping documents with customs
create >> file
## Tracking | Monitors the shipment status.
t status: tracking status
a update: update tracking status
a notify: notify client of status
update >> notify
## Compliance | Ensures adherence to legal and regulatory requirements.
t regulations: regulatory requirements
a check: check compliance
a report: report any non-compliance issues
check >> report
## cross
booking.confirm > pricing.quote
documentation.create > tracking.status
pricing.adjust > compliance.check
compliance.report > documentation.create
booking.schedule >> documentation.create
pricing.pricelist > documentation.documents
tracking.notify > booking.booking
