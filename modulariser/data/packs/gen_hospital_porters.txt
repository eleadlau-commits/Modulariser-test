# hospital porters
people: John, Sarah, Mike, Lisa, Mark
## Receiving | Processes deliveries and checks inventory. | why: supplies need to be stocked and tracked
a unload: unload deliveries
t inventory: stock inventory
a check: check delivery contents
unload > inventory
check > inventory
## Transport | Moves patients, equipment, and supplies within the hospital.
t patient: patient transport
a move: move patients
t equipment: medical equipment
move > patient
move > equipment
## Cleaning | Cleans and sanitizes patient areas and equipment.
t area: cleaning area
a clean: clean the area
t tools: cleaning tools
clean > area
clean > tools
## Restocking | Refills and maintains the supply levels in patient rooms.
t supplies: patient room supplies
a stock: restock supplies
stock > supplies
## Maintenance | Fixes and maintains equipment and facilities.
t issues: maintenance issues
a repair: repair equipment
repair > issues
## cross
receiving.unload > transport.equipment
receiving.check > restocking.supplies
transport.move > cleaning.area
cleaning.clean > maintenance.issues
restocking.stock > transport.equipment
maintenance.repair >> transport.equipment
