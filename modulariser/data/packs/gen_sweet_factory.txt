# sweet factory
people: Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave
## Sourcing | Acquires raw materials and packages. | why: raw materials are needed to produce sweets
a order: order raw materials
t suppliers: supplier list
a receive: receive deliveries
order > suppliers
order >> receive
## Production | Mixes and molds the sweets.
t ingredients: ingredient mix
a mix: mix ingredients
t molds: mold shapes
mix > ingredients
mix > molds
## Packaging | Seals and labels the sweets.
t seal: sealing machine
a seal: seal sweets
t label: label design
seal > label
seal > seal
## Quality Control | Inspects sweets for defects.
a inspect: inspect sweets
t standards: quality standards
inspect > standards
## Shipping | Prepares and dispatches orders.
t crates: shipping crates
a pack: pack orders
t manifest: shipping manifest
pack > crates
pack > manifest
## cross
sourcing.receive > production.mix
production.mix > packaging.seal
quality.inspect > packaging.seal
packaging.seal > shipping.pack
production.mix >> quality.inspect
quality.inspect >> shipping.pack
