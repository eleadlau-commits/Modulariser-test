# airline catering
people: Anna, Raj, Mei, Tom
## Menu Planning | Develops and finalizes meal plans for flights.
t menu: menu options
a review: review dietary restrictions
a update: update seasonal meals
review > menu
update > menu
## Procurement | Orders and receives food supplies.
a order: order food supplies
t suppliers: supplier list
a receive: receive food deliveries
order > suppliers
order >> receive
## Preparation | Prepares food items according to menu and safety standards.
t prep: prep list
a cook: cook food items
a package: package food items
cook >> package
cook > prep
## Assembly | Assembles meals for each flight.
t assembly: assembly checklist
a assemble: assemble meals
a label: label meals
assemble >> label
assemble > assembly
## Quality Control | Ensures meals meet safety and quality standards.
a inspect: inspect meals
a sample: sample meals
t checklist: quality checklist
inspect > checklist
sample > checklist
## cross
menu.menu > preparation.prep
menu.menu > assembly.assembly
procurement.order > preparation.cook
procurement.receive > preparation.cook
preparation.cook > quality.inspect
assembly.assemble > quality.sample
