# caravan park
people: Laura, Jake, Emma, Mike, Sarah
## Booking | Manages reservations and arrival checks.
t calendar: booking calendar
a check: check availability
a register: register bookings
check > calendar
register > calendar
## Maintenance | Checks and maintains caravans and facilities.
t list: maintenance checklist
a inspect: inspect caravans and facilities
a repair: perform necessary repairs
inspect > list
repair > list
## Cleaning | Cleans and prepares caravans for guests.
t schedule: cleaning schedule
a clean: clean caravans
a prep: prepare caravans for guests
clean > schedule
prep > schedule
## Reception | Welcomes guests and handles check-in/check-out.
t log: guest log
a greet: greet and welcome guests
a process: process check-in/check-out
greet > log
process > log
## Security | Monitors the site and responds to emergencies.
t log: security log
a patrol: patrol the premises
a respond: respond to emergencies
patrol > log
respond > log
## cross
booking.check > cleaning.schedule
booking.register > maintenance.list
maintenance.repair >> cleaning.clean
reception.greet >> booking.check
reception.process >> booking.calendar
security.patrol > reception.log
