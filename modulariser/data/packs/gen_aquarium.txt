# aquarium
people: Alice, Bob, Charlie, Dana
## Water Management | Monitors and maintains water quality.
t tests: water test results
a test: test water parameters
a adjust: adjust water chemistry
test >> adjust
## Feeding | Plans and executes feeding schedules.
t schedule: feeding schedule
a feed: feed the fish
a observe: observe feeding behavior
feed > schedule
feed >> observe
## Maintenance | Cleans and inspects equipment.
t checklist: maintenance checklist
a clean: clean aquarium and equipment
a inspect: inspect equipment
clean >> inspect
inspect > checklist
## Stock Management | Tracks and manages fish stock.
t inventory: fish inventory
a count: count the fish
a tag: tag new arrivals
count > inventory
tag > inventory
## Health Monitoring | Tracks fish health and disease.
t logs: health logs
a inspect: visually inspect fish
a treat: treat sick fish
inspect > logs
treat > logs
## cross
water.test > stock.inventory
water.adjust >> feeding.feed
maintenance.clean > water.tests
stock.count > health.logs
feeding.observe >> health.inspect
health.treat >> stock.tag
