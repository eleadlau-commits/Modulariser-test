# short film production
people: Zara, Elliot, Mo, June
## Development | Writes and finances the script. | why: there must be a script
t script: shooting script
a rewrite: rewrite the script
t budget: production budget
rewrite > script
## Casting | Finds the actors.
a auditions: hold auditions
t castlist: cast list
auditions > castlist
## Locations | Finds and secures places to film.
a scout: scout locations
t permits: filming permits
scout >> permits
## Shoot | Films the scenes.
t callsheet: call sheet
a film: film the scenes
t schedule: shooting schedule
film > callsheet
callsheet > schedule
## Post | Edits and finishes the film.
a editcut: edit the cut
a grade: colour grade
a sound: mix sound
editcut >> grade
editcut >> sound
## cross
casting.auditions > development.script
shoot.schedule > development.script
shoot.callsheet > casting.castlist
shoot.callsheet > locations.permits
shoot.film >> post.editcut
locations.scout > development.budget
