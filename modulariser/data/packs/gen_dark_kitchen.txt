# dark kitchen
people: Jake, Mia, Luis, Zoe
## Order Management | Handles incoming orders and ensures accuracy. | why: orders need to be processed and tracked
a receive: receive orders
a verify: verify order details
t queue: order queue
receive >> verify
verify > queue
## Inventory Management | Tracks and manages ingredient stock. | why: ensures ingredients are available
t stock: inventory stock
a check: check ingredient levels
a restock: restock ingredients
check > stock
restock > stock
## Preparation | Prepares ingredients and meals based on orders.
t prep: prep list
a prep: prepare ingredients
a cook: cook meals
prep > prep
prep >> cook
## Quality Control | Ensures meals meet quality standards before packaging.
a inspect: inspect meals
a package: package meals
t standard: quality standards
inspect > standard
inspect >> package
## Dispatch | Packages and dispatches orders to customers.
a pack: pack meals
a dispatch: dispatch orders
t delivery: delivery list
pack >> dispatch
dispatch > delivery
## cross
order.queue > preparation.prep
order.queue > quality.inspect
inventory.restock > preparation.prep
inventory.check > preparation.prep
quality.package > dispatch.dispatch
preparation.cook > quality.inspect
