# skip hire company
people: Lisa, Mike, Sarah, Tom, Kevin
## Booking | Coordinates and confirms customer bookings.
t calendar: booking calendar
a confirm: confirm booking details
a assign: assign equipment based on booking
confirm > calendar
assign > calendar
## Delivery | Prepares and transports the skips to the job site.
a load: load skips onto trucks
a transport: transport skips to site
t manifest: delivery manifest
load >> transport
load > manifest
## Setup | Prepares the site and places the skips.
t siteplan: site plan
a siteprep: prepare the site
a place: place skips according to plan
siteprep > siteplan
siteprep >> place
place > siteplan
## Monitoring | Tracks the use and condition of the skips.
a inspect: inspect skips daily
t log: usage log
a report: report any issues
inspect > log
report > log
## Collection | Prepares and transports the skips back to the depot.
a collect: collect skips from site
a unload: unload skips at depot
t checklist: collection checklist
collect >> unload
collect > checklist
## cross
booking.calendar > delivery.manifest
booking.assign >> setup.place
setup.place > monitoring.log
setup.siteprep > monitoring.log
monitoring.report >> collection.collect
delivery.transport >> setup.siteprep
collection.unload > delivery.manifest
