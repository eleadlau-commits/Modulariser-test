t schedule: event schedule
a coordinate: coordinate with venue
t guestlist: guest list
coordinate > schedule

## Religious Preparation | Prepares religious elements and readings. | why: religious events require spiritual preparation
t readings: Torah readings
a prepare: prepare for service
t blessings: blessings list
prepare > readings
prepare > blessings
## Catering | Manages food and beverages for the event.
t menu: catering menu
a order: order food and beverages
a deliver: delivery of food
order > menu
order >> deliver
## Decoration | Sets up the venue and manages decor.
t decor: decorations
a set: set up venue
a remove: remove decorations
set > decor
set >> remove
## Security | Ensures safety and security measures are in place.
t checklist: security checklist
a patrol: patrol the venue
a monitor: monitor entry points
patrol > checklist
monitor > checklist
