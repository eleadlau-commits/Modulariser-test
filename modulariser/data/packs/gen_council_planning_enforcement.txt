# council planning enforcement
people: Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave
## Complaints | Receives and logs complaints about planning issues. | why: complaints drive the enforcement process
t logs: complaints log
a register: register complaints
a investigate: investigate complaints
register >> investigate
investigate > logs
## Inspections | Conducts site visits and inspections. | why: ensures compliance with planning permissions
t report: inspection report
a visit: visit site
a document: document findings
visit >> document
document > report
## Enforcement | Takes action against breaches, including notices and penalties. | why: ensures legal compliance
t notice: enforcement notice
a issue: issue notice
a followup: follow up on compliance
issue >> followup
issue > notice
## Appeals | Manages the appeal process against enforcement actions.
t appeal: appeal record
a register: register appeals
a review: review appeal
register >> review
review > appeal
## Legal | Prepares for and manages legal proceedings if necessary.
t case: legal case
a prepare: prepare for hearings
a prosecute: prosecute cases
prepare >> prosecute
prosecute > case
## cross
complaints.register > inspections.visit
complaints.investigate >> enforcement.issue
inspections.document >> enforcement.issue
enforcement.followup >> appeals.register
legal.prepare >> enforcement.issue
legal.prosecute > enforcement.notice
