# orchestra
people: Maria, Liam, Clara, Ethan, Sophia
## Repertoire | Chooses and prepares the music for performances. | why: concert programs need to be decided and prepared
t program: concert program
a curate: curate the music selection
t scores: sheet music
curate > program
curate > scores
## Rehearsal | Practices the music to ensure polished performance. | why: rehearsals improve performance quality
a practice: practice the music
t schedule: rehearsal schedule
practice > schedule
## Conducting | Leads the orchestra through the music. | why: a conductor directs the ensemble
t baton: conductor's baton
a conduct: conduct rehearsals and performances
conduct > baton
## Performance | Executes the music in front of an audience.
t stage: concert stage
a perform: perform the concert
perform > stage
## Maintenance | Ensures instruments are in working order. | why: instruments must be in good condition
a tune: tune instruments
t tools: maintenance tools
tune > tools
## cross
repertoire.curate > rehearsal.practice
repertoire.scores > conducting.conduct
conducting.conduct > performance.perform
maintenance.tune > rehearsal.practice
maintenance.tools > performance.perform
