# kennel and cattery
people: Mia, Ethan, Lila, Noah, Harper
## Intake | Evaluates and records new animals coming into the kennel or cattery.
a assess: assess new animals
t records: intake records
a vaccinate: vaccinate new animals
assess >> vaccinate
vaccinate > records
## Housing | Assigns and maintains the living spaces for the animals.
t cages: animal cages
a assign: assign animals to cages
a clean: clean cages daily
assign > cages
clean > cages
## Feeding | Prepares and delivers food to the animals.
t menu: feeding schedule
a prepare: prepare food
a deliver: deliver food to cages
prepare >> deliver
deliver > menu
## Exercise | Ensures animals get regular exercise and socialization.
a walk: walk dogs
a play: play with cats
t schedule: exercise schedule
walk > schedule
play > schedule
## Health | Monitors and treats the health of the animals.
t logs: health logs
a check: regularly check on animals
a treat: administer treatments
check > logs
treat > logs
## Release | Preps animals for adoption or return to owners.
t forms: release forms
a prep: prepare animals for release
a release: release animals to new owners
prep >> release
release > forms
## cross
intake.assess > housing.assign
intake.vaccinate > health.logs
housing.assign > feeding.deliver
exercise.walk > health.check
health.check > release.prep
feeding.menu > health.check
