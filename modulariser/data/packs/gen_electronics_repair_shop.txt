# electronics repair shop
people: Mike, Sarah, Lucas, Emily, Jordan
## Reception | Manages incoming repairs and customer interactions. | why: essential for customer service and tracking repairs
t log: repair log
a intake: intake devices
t status: repair status
intake > log
intake > status
## Diagnostics | Identifies the problems with the devices.
a test: run diagnostic tests
t report: diagnostic report
test > report
## Repair | Fixes the identified issues. | why: necessary to restore device functionality
t part: replacement parts
a fix: perform repairs
a calibrate: calibrate device
fix > part
fix >> calibrate
## Quality Check | Ensures repairs meet the standards. | why: ensures customer satisfaction and reliability
a inspect: inspect repaired devices
t checklist: quality checklist
inspect > checklist
## Return | Prepares and delivers repaired devices back to customers.
t receipt: delivery receipt
a prepare: prepare for delivery
a deliver: deliver to customer
prepare > receipt
deliver > receipt
## cross
reception.intake > diagnostics.test
diagnostics.report > repair.fix
repair.calibrate > quality.inspect
quality.inspect >> return.prepare
repair.part > quality.checklist
return.receipt > reception.status
