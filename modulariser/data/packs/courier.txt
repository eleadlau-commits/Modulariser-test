# courier depot
people: Vic, Hamid, Paula, Den
## Collections | Picks parcels up from senders. | why: parcels must be collected
a book: book collections
a pickup: pick up parcels
book >> pickup
## Sorting | Sorts parcels by route.
a scan: scan parcels in
a sort: sort into routes
t sortplan: sort plan
scan >> sort
sort > sortplan
## Routing | Plans driver routes.
t routes: route sheets
a plan: plan routes
plan > routes
## Delivery | Delivers parcels.
a deliver: deliver parcels
t pod: proof of delivery
deliver > pod
## Fleet | Keeps vans running.
t vans: van schedule
a servicevan: service vans
servicevan > vans
## cross
collections.pickup >> sorting.scan
sorting.sortplan > routing.routes
delivery.deliver > routing.routes
routing.plan > fleet.vans
delivery.pod > sorting.scan
