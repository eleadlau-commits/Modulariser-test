# charity fundraising
people: Bea, Oscar, Leila, Dan
## Donors | Keeps donor details and history. | why: donations need a donor record
t crm: donor database
a thank: send thank-you letters
thank > crm
## Campaigns | Plans appeals and events.
a plan: plan the appeal
t calendar: campaign calendar
a mailout: send the appeal mailing
plan > calendar
plan >> mailout
## Giving | Takes donations in.
a online: take online donations
a giftaid: claim Gift Aid
t ledger: donations ledger
online > ledger
giftaid > ledger
## Volunteers | Recruits and rosters volunteers.
t rota: volunteer rota
a recruit: recruit volunteers
recruit >> rota
## Reporting | Reports to trustees.
a report: write the trustee report
t kpis: fundraising dashboard
report > kpis
## cross
campaigns.mailout > donors.crm
giving.online > donors.crm
donors.thank > giving.ledger
campaigns.plan > volunteers.rota
reporting.kpis > giving.ledger
reporting.report > campaigns.calendar
