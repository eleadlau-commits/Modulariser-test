# council parking enforcement
people: Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave
## Ticketing | Issues tickets for parking violations.
a spot: spot violations
t ticket: ticket
a issue: issue tickets
spot >> issue
issue > ticket
## Appeals | Handles appeals and resolutions.
t appeal: appeal form
a review: review appeals
a resolve: resolve appeals
review >> resolve
resolve > appeal
## Collection | Collects fines and manages payment.
a fine: issue fines
t payment: payment record
a collect: collect payments
fine >> collect
collect > payment
## Investigation | Investigates serious or repeat violations.
a track: track repeat offenders
t report: violation report
a investigate: conduct investigations
track >> investigate
investigate > report
## Compliance | Ensures adherence to regulations and policies.
t policy: parking policy
a audit: audit enforcement
a update: update policies
audit > policy
update > policy
## cross
ticketing.spot > investigation.track
investigation.report > ticketing.issue
ticketing.ticket > collection.fine
ticketing.ticket > appeals.review
collection.payment > ticketing.issue
compliance.update >> ticketing.issue
compliance.audit >> ticketing.issue
