# convenience store
people: Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave
## Ordering | Ensures necessary stock is available for sale.
a place: place orders
t vendors: vendor list
a track: track order status
place > vendors
place >> track
## Stock Management | Keeps track of what is in stock and where.
t inventory: stock inventory
a count: count stock daily
a restock: restock shelves
count > inventory
restock > inventory
## Sales | Handles customer transactions and maintains sales records.
t register: cash register
a scan: scan items
a pay: process payment
scan >> pay
pay > register
## Security | Ensures the store is safe and secure.
a patrol: patrol store
t cameras: security cameras
a monitor: monitor security footage
patrol > cameras
monitor > cameras
## Cleaning | Maintains a clean and pleasant store environment.
t schedule: cleaning schedule
a sweep: sweep floors
a wipe: wipe surfaces
sweep > schedule
wipe > schedule
## cross
ordering.place > stock.inventory
stock.restock > sales.scan
security.monitor >> cleaning.sweep
sales.pay > security.monitor
stock.count > security.patrol
