# tea room
people: Linda, John, Sarah, Peter
## Sourcing | Procures high-quality tea and accessories. | why: ensures the tea room has the best products
a select: select tea varieties
t suppliers: supplier contacts
a order: place orders
select >> order
order > suppliers
## Inventory | Manages tea and accessory stock. | why: keeps track of available stock
t ledger: inventory ledger
a count: count inventory monthly
a update: update stock levels
count > ledger
update > ledger
## Preparation | Prepares tea and serves customers.
t menu: tea menu
a brew: brew tea
a serve: serve tea
brew > menu
serve > brew
## Cleaning | Ensures the tea room is clean and welcoming.
t schedule: cleaning schedule
a wipe: wipe surfaces
a sweep: sweep floors
wipe > schedule
sweep > schedule
## Accounting | Handles financial transactions and reports. | why: tracks the financial health of the tea room
a record: record transactions
t ledger: accounting ledger
a report: generate financial reports
record >> ledger
report > ledger
## cross
sourcing.order > inventory.ledger
sourcing.select > preparation.menu
inventory.update > preparation.brew
preparation.serve >> cleaning.wipe
accounting.record > sourcing.order
accounting.record > inventory.update
