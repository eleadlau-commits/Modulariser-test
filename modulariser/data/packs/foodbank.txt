# food bank
people: Rev Clarke, Shona, Abdi, Win
## Donations | Collects food from donors. | why: food has to come in
a collect: collect from supermarkets
a weigh: weigh donations
t donorlog: donations log
collect >> weigh
weigh > donorlog
## Warehouse | Sorts and stores food.
a sort: sort food by date
t shelves: stock shelves
sort > shelves
## Referrals | Takes referrals from agencies.
t vouchers: referral vouchers
a check: check vouchers
check > vouchers
## Distribution | Hands out parcels.
a packparcel: pack food parcels
a handout: hand out parcels
t parcellog: parcel log
packparcel >> handout
handout > parcellog
## Volunteers | Rosters helpers.
t rota: volunteer rota
a train: train new volunteers
## cross
warehouse.sort > donations.donorlog
distribution.packparcel > warehouse.shelves
distribution.handout > referrals.vouchers
distribution.packparcel > volunteers.rota
donations.collect > volunteers.rota
