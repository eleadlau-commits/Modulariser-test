# minicab dispatch
people: Alex, Bella, Chris, Dana
## Booking | Receives and confirms ride requests.
a receive: receive booking requests
t bookings: booking log
a confirm: confirm bookings
receive >> bookings
confirm > bookings
## Dispatch | Assigns drivers to bookings.
a assign: assign drivers to bookings
t availability: driver availability list
a notify: notify drivers of bookings
assign > availability
assign >> notify
## Tracking | Tracks driver locations and ride progress.
t locations: driver location updates
a track: track driver progress
t status: ride status updates
track > locations
track >> status
## Support | Handles rider and driver support requests.
a handle: handle support requests
t issues: issue log
a resolve: resolve issues
handle >> issues
resolve > issues
## Reporting | Compiles and presents operational data.
a compile: compile daily reports
t data: operational data
a present: present reports
compile > data
compile >> present
## cross
booking.confirm > dispatch.assign
dispatch.notify > tracking.track
tracking.status > reporting.data
support.handle > reporting.data
support.resolve > booking.bookings
