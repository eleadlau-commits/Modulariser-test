# fishmonger
people: Olivia, Liam, Isabella, Ethan
## Sourcing | Procures fresh fish from suppliers.
a source: source fish
t suppliers: supplier list
a inspect: inspect quality
source >> inspect
source > suppliers
## Storage | Keeps fish fresh and tracks inventory.
t inventory: inventory log
a store: store fish
a check: check freshness
store > inventory
check > inventory
## Preparation | Prepares fish for sale.
t cuts: cut types
a clean: clean fish
a cut: cut fish
clean >> cut
cut > cuts
## Sales | Sells fish to customers.
t customers: customer list
a sell: sell fish
a service: service customers
sell > customers
service > customers
## Accounting | Tracks expenses and revenues.
a record: record sales
t ledger: sales ledger
a reconcile: reconcile accounts
record > ledger
reconcile > ledger
## cross
sourcing.source > storage.store
sourcing.inspect >> storage.check
preparation.cut >> sales.sell
storage.inventory > preparation.clean
sales.sell > accounting.record
accounting.reconcile >> sourcing.suppliers
