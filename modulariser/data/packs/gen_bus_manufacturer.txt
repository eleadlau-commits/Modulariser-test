# bus manufacturer
people: John, Emma, Leo, Ava
## Design | Creates the blueprint and specifications of the bus.
t schematics: design schematics
a model: create 3D models
a spec: write technical specifications
model >> spec
spec > schematics
## Engineering | Builds and tests the prototype.
a build: build the prototype
a test: perform initial tests
t prototype: prototype bus
build >> test
test > prototype
## Manufacturing | Produces the parts and assembles the bus.
t parts: list of required parts
a assemble: assemble the bus
a inspect: final bus inspection
assemble > parts
inspect > assemble
## Quality Control | Ensures the bus meets safety and quality standards.
a inspect: perform quality inspections
t checklist: quality checklist
a adjust: make necessary adjustments
inspect > checklist
adjust > inspect
## Sales | Manages the sales process and customer relations.
t leads: list of sales leads
a quote: provide quotes
a demo: conduct demonstrations
quote > leads
demo >> quote
## cross
design.spec > engineering.prototype
design.schematics > manufacturing.parts
engineering.test >> manufacturing.assemble
quality.inspect >> sales.quote
sales.demo >> quality.adjust
