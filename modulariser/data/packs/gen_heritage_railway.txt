# heritage railway
people: Emma, Liam, Noah, Olivia, Isabella
## Maintenance | Ensures the locomotives and tracks are in working order. | why: safety and operational readiness
a inspect: inspect locomotives and tracks
a repair: repair any damages
t records: maintenance records
inspect >> repair
repair > records
## Scheduling | Plans the train runs and ensures timetables are followed. | why: to maintain a consistent service
t timetable: train timetable
a schedule: schedule train runs
a coordinate: coordinate with stations
schedule > timetable
coordinate > timetable
## Ticketing | Sells tickets and manages the booking process.
t bookings: ticket bookings
a sell: sell tickets
a check: check bookings
sell > bookings
check > bookings
## Operations | Manages the actual running of the trains.
t roster: crew roster
a run: operate the train
a communicate: communicate with control
run > roster
communicate > run
## Preservation | Keeps historical artefacts and documents.
t archive: historical archive
a document: document historical events
a store: store historical artefacts
document > archive
store > archive
## cross
maintenance.repair > operations.run
scheduling.schedule > operations.run
ticketing.sell > operations.run
preservation.document > maintenance.records
preservation.store > maintenance.records
