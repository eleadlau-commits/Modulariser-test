# dental clinic
people: Dr Patel, Grace, Owen, Fatima
## Reception | Books appointments and checks patients in. | why: patients need a way to get appointments
t diary: appointment diary
a book: book appointments
a checkin: check patients in
book > diary
checkin > diary
## Records | Keeps patient history and consent.
t notes: patient notes
t consent: consent forms
a update: update medical history
update > notes
## Treatment | Examines and treats patients.
a examine: examine the patient
a treat: carry out treatment
t plan: treatment plan
examine >> treat
treat > plan
## Sterilising | Cleans and sterilises instruments.
a clean: clean instruments
a autoclave: run the autoclave
t log: sterilisation log
clean >> autoclave
autoclave > log
## Payments | Bills patients and claims from insurers.
a invoice: invoice the patient
a claim: submit insurance claims
t price: fee schedule
invoice > price
## cross
reception.checkin >> treatment.examine
treatment.examine > records.notes
treatment.treat > records.consent
treatment.plan > records.notes
treatment.treat > sterilising.log
payments.invoice > treatment.plan
payments.claim > records.notes
