# charity shop
people: Emma, John, Lisa, Michael, Sarah
## Donations | Receives and sorts donated items. | why: the shop needs items to sell
a receive: receive donations
a sort: sort donations
t log: donation log
receive >> sort
sort > log
## Inventory | Tracks what items are available and their condition. | why: to manage the stock effectively
t list: inventory list
a check: check stock condition
check > list
## Pricing | Sets prices for items based on their condition and demand. | why: items need to be priced for sale
a assess: assess item condition
a price: set price
t guide: pricing guide
assess >> price
price > guide
## Sales | Handles customer interactions and transactions. | why: to sell items and raise funds
t register: sales register
a assist: assist customers
a transact: process sales
assist > register
transact > register
## Cleaning | Cleans and prepares items for display. | why: to present items attractively
a clean: clean items
a prepare: prepare items for display
t schedule: cleaning schedule
clean >> prepare
prepare > schedule
## cross
donations.receive > inventory.list
donations.sort > inventory.list
inventory.check > pricing.assess
pricing.price > sales.register
cleaning.prepare > sales.assist
sales.transact > cleaning.schedule
