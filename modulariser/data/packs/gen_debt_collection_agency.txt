# debt collection agency
people: Sam, Lisa, Mike, Nora
## Case Assignment | Assigns cases to collectors based on criteria. | why: ensures efficient distribution of workload
a assign: assign cases to collectors
t criteria: assignment criteria
a review: review case details
assign > criteria
review > assign
## Collection | Communicates with debtors to negotiate payments. | why: aims to recover debts
a contact: contact debtors
t payment: payment plan
a negotiate: negotiate terms
contact > payment
negotiate > payment
## Documentation | Maintains records and documentation of all actions taken. | why: critical for legal compliance and auditing
t file: case file
a update: update case file
a log: log communications
update > file
log > file
## Legal | Prepares and initiates legal actions if necessary. | why: ensures legal recourse for uncooperative debtors
t lawsuit: legal case filing
a prepare: prepare legal documentation
a file: file lawsuit
prepare > lawsuit
file > lawsuit
## Reporting | Compiles reports on recovery rates and collector performance. | why: supports management decisions and performance tracking
t report: performance report
a compile: compile recovery data
a analyze: analyze performance
compile > report
analyze > report
## cross
case.assign > collection.contact
collection.negotiate > documentation.update
collection.negotiate >> documentation.log
legal.file >> reporting.compile
collection.contact >> reporting.analyze
documentation.update > reporting.compile
legal.prepare > collection.negotiate
