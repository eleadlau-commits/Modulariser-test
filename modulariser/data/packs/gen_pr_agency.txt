## Strategy | Defines the campaign goals and messages. | why: clear goals and messages are essential for effective PR.
a plan: plan the campaign
t plan: campaign plan
a message: define key messages
message > plan
plan >> message
## Media | Identifies and engages media outlets. | why: media coverage amplifies the message.
a research: research media outlets
t contacts: media contact list
a pitch: pitch stories to media
pitch > contacts
research >> pitch
## Content | Creates press releases, statements, and other materials. | why: quality content is crucial for media engagement.
t release: press release
a write: write press materials
a review: review content for accuracy
review > release
write >> review
## Monitoring | Tracks media coverage and public reaction. | why: feedback is essential for adjusting the campaign.
a track: track media coverage
t report: coverage report
a analyze: analyze public sentiment
analyze > report
track >> analyze
## Client | Manages client expectations and communication. | why: keeping the client informed is vital for a successful campaign.
a brief: provide briefs to client
t updates: client updates
a feedback: gather client feedback
brief > updates
feedback > updates
## cross
