# recycling centre
people: Mia, Carlos, Linda, Jack, Olivia
## Reception | Identifies and sorts incoming waste. | why: waste needs to be identified and sorted for processing
a check: check waste quality
t schedule: delivery schedule
a sort: sort waste into categories
check >> sort
## Processing | Breaks down waste into reusable materials.
a shred: shred waste
a wash: wash materials
t bins: processing bins
shred >> wash
wash > bins
## Quality Control | Ensures materials meet standards before storage.
a inspect: inspect materials
t defects: defect log
a approve: approve for storage
inspect >> approve
inspect > defects
## Storage | Stores processed materials until needed.
t racks: storage racks
a store: store materials
a track: track inventory levels
store > racks
track > racks
## Distribution | Prepares and ships materials to buyers.
a pack: pack materials
t orders: order list
a ship: ship materials
pack >> ship
pack > orders
## cross
reception.sort > processing.shred
quality.inspect > processing.wash
processing.wash > quality.inspect
quality.approve > storage.store
storage.track > distribution.pack
distribution.orders > storage.track
