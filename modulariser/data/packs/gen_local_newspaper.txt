# local newspaper
people: Emma, David, Lisa, Carlos, Brian
## Reporting | Writes stories and gathers information.
a interview: conduct interviews
a research: research topics
t notes: reporter notes
interview >> research
interview > notes
## Editing | Checks and formats the stories.
a proofread: proofread articles
a format: format articles for print
t guide: style guide
proofread >> format
proofread > guide
## Layout | Assembles the pages for the newspaper.
t template: layout template
a arrange: arrange articles on page
t images: image library
arrange > template
arrange > images
## Printing | Prepares and prints the final edition.
a prep: prepare the press
a print: print the newspaper
t edition: final edition files
prep >> print
prep > edition
## Delivery | Distributes the newspaper to subscribers and stands.
a load: load the trucks
a deliver: deliver newspapers
t route: delivery route map
load >> deliver
load > route
## cross
reporting.research > editing.proofread
reporting.notes > editing.proofread
editing.format > layout.arrange
layout.arrange > printing.prep
printing.print > delivery.load
delivery.deliver > printing.print
