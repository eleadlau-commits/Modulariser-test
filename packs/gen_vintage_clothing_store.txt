# vintage clothing store
people: Lily, Ethan, Olivia, Noah, Mia
## Sourcing | Finds and selects vintage clothing items. | why: stock has to be sourced from somewhere
a scout: scout for clothing items
t vendors: vendor list
a purchase: purchase selected items
scout > vendors
scout >> purchase
## Inventory | Tracks and manages the stock of clothing items.
t ledger: inventory ledger
a count: count inventory monthly
count > ledger
## Cleaning | Cleans and prepares the items for display. | why: clothing must be clean and presentable
t supplies: cleaning supplies
a wash: wash clothing items
a dry: dry clothing items
wash >> dry
wash > supplies
## Display | Arranges items in the store and online. | why: items need to be visible to customers
t tags: price tags
a arrange: arrange items in store
a list: list items online
list > tags
arrange > tags
## Sales | Handles customer transactions and maintains records. | why: sales need to be recorded and customers served
a serve: serve customers
t receipts: sales receipts
a record: record sales
serve >> record
record > receipts
## cross
sourcing.purchase > inventory.ledger
inventory.count > sourcing.scout
cleaning.wash > display.arrange
cleaning.wash > display.list
sales.serve >> inventory.ledger
sales.record > inventory.ledger
