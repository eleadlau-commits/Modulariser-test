schedule > calendar

## Setup | Prepares the campsite for guests. | why: ensures the campsite is ready for guests
a clean: clean campsite areas
a set: set up campsite facilities
t supplies: supply inventory
clean >> set

## Catering | Prepares and serves meals to guests. | why: ensures guests are fed

a serve: serve meals

## Maintenance | Checks and maintains the campsite facilities. 
a inspect: inspect facilities
a repair: repair facilities
t log: maintenance log
inspect >> repair
inspect > log

a patrol: patrol campsite areas
t emergency: emergency contact list

patrol >> respond
respond > emergency
