# cycling club
people: Max, Zoe, Liam, Olivia, Noah
## Membership | Manages new and existing members.
t roster: member roster
a register: register new members
t dues: membership dues
register > roster
register >> dues
## Training | Organizes weekly training rides.
t schedule: training schedule
a lead: lead training rides
a track: track rider progress
lead > schedule
lead >> track
## Events | Coordinates club participation in races and tours.
t calendar: event calendar
a recruit: recruit riders for events
a enter: enter events officially
recruit > calendar
enter > calendar
## Sponsorship | Secures and manages financial support.
t sponsors: sponsor list
a negotiate: negotiate sponsorship deals
t budget: sponsorship budget
negotiate > sponsors
negotiate >> budget
## Social | Organizes social gatherings and club nights.
t agenda: social agenda
a organize: organize social events
a invite: invite members to events
organize > agenda
invite > agenda
## cross
membership.roster > training.schedule
membership.dues > sponsorship.budget
events.calendar >> training.track
sponsorship.budget >> social.agenda
social.invite > membership.roster
