# parish council
people: Sarah, Mike, Lisa, John
## Meetings | Organizes and conducts regular meetings.
t agenda: meeting agenda
a call: call to order
a discuss: discuss items
a adjourn: adjourn meeting
call > agenda
call >> discuss
discuss >> adjourn
## Finances | Manages the financial operations and budget.
t budget: annual budget
a collect: collect payments
a disburse: disburse funds
a audit: perform internal audits
collect >> budget
disburse > budget
audit > budget
## Community Projects | Initiates and oversees local projects.
t proposals: project proposals
a review: review proposals
a implement: implement projects
a report: report progress
review > proposals
implement > proposals
report > implement
## Communications | Manages communications with the community.
t newsletter: parish newsletter
a publicize: publicize events
a respond: respond to inquiries
a update: update the parish website
publicize > newsletter
respond > newsletter
update > newsletter
## Planning | Plans for future needs and improvements.
t plans: development plans
a consult: consult with stakeholders
a draft: draft plans
a approve: approve plans
consult > plans
draft > plans
draft >> approve
## cross
meetings.agenda > finances.budget
meetings.agenda > community.proposals
meetings.agenda > planning.plans
communications.publicize > community.implement
community.report > meetings.adjourn
