# book publisher
people: Alex, Mia, Ryan, Casey, Lisa
## Acquisitions | Identifies and acquires new book proposals. | why: new content is needed to publish books
a scout: scout for new authors and proposals
t proposals: list of book proposals
a evaluate: evaluate the proposals for acquisition
scout > proposals
evaluate > proposals
## Editing | Refines and edits the book manuscripts. | why: ensures the book quality
a proofread: proofread the manuscript
a edit: edit the manuscript
t manuscript: book manuscript
proofread > manuscript
edit > manuscript
## Design | Designs the book cover and layout. | why: creates an appealing visual presentation
a layout: design layout
a cover: design cover
t mockup: book design mockup
layout > mockup
cover > mockup
## Production | Prepares the book for printing. | why: needs to prepare for actual printing
a print: prepare for printing
a format: format for printing
t printfile: print-ready file
print > printfile
format > printfile
## Marketing | Promotes the book to potential readers.
a promote: promote the book
t campaign: marketing campaign
a advertise: place advertisements
promote > campaign
advertise > campaign
## Sales | Sells the book through various channels.
a distribute: distribute to stores
t orders: sales orders
a track: track sales
distribute > orders
track > orders
## cross
acquisitions.proposals > editing.manuscript
design.mockup > production.printfile
marketing.promote >> sales.distribute
sales.orders >> production.print
editing.edit >> design.mockup
