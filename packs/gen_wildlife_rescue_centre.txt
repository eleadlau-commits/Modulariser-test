# wildlife rescue centre
people: Sarah, Michael, Lisa, Tom, Emma
## Intake | Receives and initially assesses the animals.
t form: intake form
a examine: examine the animal
a triage: prioritize care based on urgency
examine > form
examine >> triage
## Medical | Provides treatment and monitoring.
a treat: administer treatment
t records: medical records
a monitor: monitor recovery
t medications: medication list
treat >> monitor
monitor > records
treat > medications
## Rehabilitation | Prepares animals for release back into the wild.
t plan: rehabilitation plan
a train: train animals for survival
a assess: assess readiness for release
train >> assess
assess > plan
## Release | Returns animals to their natural habitat.
a release: release into the wild
t location: release location
a track: track post-release survival
release >> track
track > location
## Education | Educates the public about wildlife conservation.
a talk: give educational talks
t materials: educational materials
a workshop: conduct workshops
talk > materials
workshop > materials
## cross
intake.examine > medical.records
intake.triage >> medical.treat
medical.monitor > rehabilitation.train
rehabilitation.assess >> release.release
education.talk >> intake.form
education.workshop >> medical.records
