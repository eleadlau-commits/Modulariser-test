# butcher shop
people: Mike, Sarah, Tom, Laura, David
## Sourcing | Procures meat and supplies from suppliers. | why: meat has to be sourced from somewhere
a order: order meat supplies
t suppliers: supplier list
a receive: receive deliveries
order > suppliers
order >> receive
## Butchery | Cuts meat into various portions and types.
t cuts: cut list
a cut: cut meat
a wrap: wrap meat portions
cut >> wrap
cut > cuts
## Display | Sets up meat for sale and maintains freshness.
t display: meat display
a prep: prepare display
a rotate: rotate inventory
prep > display
rotate > display
## Sales | Manages customer transactions and orders.
t register: cash register
a serve: serve customers
a record: record sales
serve >> record
serve > register
## Inventory | Tracks stock levels and orders restocks.
t stock: stock levels
a count: count stock daily
a reorder: reorder stock
count > stock
count >> reorder
## cross
sourcing.order > inventory.stock
sourcing.receive > inventory.stock
butchery.cut > display.display
butchery.wrap > sales.serve
sales.serve >> inventory.count
