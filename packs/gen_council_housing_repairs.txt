# council housing repairs
people: Sarah, John, Emily, Michael, Lisa
## Reporting | Collects and logs repair requests. | why: repairs can't start without a request
a log: log repair requests
t form: repair request form
a validate: validate request details
log > form
log >> validate
## Scheduling | Assigns technicians and dates for repairs. | why: ensures repairs are timely and efficient
a assign: assign technicians
t calendar: repair schedule
a confirm: confirm appointments
assign > calendar
confirm > calendar
## Inspection | Assesses the extent of damage and materials needed. | why: necessary for accurate repair planning
a examine: examine the damage
t report: damage report
a estimate: estimate materials
examine >> report
estimate > report
## Repair | Performs the actual repair works. | why: the core of the repair process
a fix: perform repair
t tool: repair tools
t material: repair materials
fix > material
fix > tool
## Documentation | Records the completion and updates systems. | why: important for tracking and compliance
a complete: mark repair as complete
t record: repair completion record
a update: update property database
complete >> record
update > record
## cross
reporting.log > scheduling.calendar
inspection.examine >> repair.fix
repair.fix >> documentation.complete
scheduling.assign >> inspection.examine
documentation.update >> reporting.log
