# tree nursery
people: Emma, Liam, Olivia, Noah, Ava
## Propagation | Grows seedlings from seeds or cuttings. | why: nursery needs seedlings to grow into mature trees
a sow: sow seeds
t trays: seed trays
a pot: pot seedlings
sow > trays
sow >> pot
## Nurturing | Cares for young plants until they reach maturity.
t nursery: plant nursery
a water: water plants
a fertilize: fertilize plants
water > nursery
fertilize > nursery
## Inventory | Tracks the number of trees and their species.
t ledger: inventory ledger
a count: count trees
a species: record species
count > ledger
species > ledger
## Sales | Sells trees to customers.
t catalog: sales catalog
a order: process orders
a deliver: deliver trees
order > catalog
order >> deliver
## Maintenance | Ensures the nursery is clean and organized.
t tools: maintenance tools
a clean: clean nursery
a organize: organize space
clean > tools
organize > tools
## cross
propagation.pot > nurturing.nursery
nurturing.water > inventory.ledger
nurturing.fertilize >> sales.order
inventory.count > sales.catalog
maintenance.clean >> propagation.trays
maintenance.organize > sales.deliver
