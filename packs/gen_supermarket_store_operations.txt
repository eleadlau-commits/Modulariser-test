# supermarket store operations
people: Sarah, Mike, Linda, Tom, Robert
## Receiving and Stocking | Receives deliveries and stocks items. | why: products need to be in place for sales.
a unloading: unload delivery trucks
t stock: inventory list
a restocking: restock shelves
unloading > stock
unloading >> restocking
## Inventory Management | Manages stock levels and updates inventory. | why: to avoid stockouts and overstocking.
t system: inventory management system
a count: count stock periodically
a update: update inventory system
count > system
update > system
## Sales Floor Management | Organizes the sales floor and customer service.
t floorplan: sales floor layout
a arrange: arrange display
a assist: assist customers
arrange > floorplan
assist > floorplan
## Checkout Operations | Handles transactions and customer billing.
a scan: scan items at checkout
t receipt: receipt
a pay: accept payment
scan >> pay
pay > receipt
## Loss Prevention | Prevents theft and shrinkage. | why: to protect store profits.
t surveillance: security system
a patrol: patrol the sales floor
a check: check for shrinkage
patrol > surveillance
check > surveillance
## cross
receiving.unloading > inventory.system
inventory.count > receiving.stock
sales.arrange > receiving.stock
checkout.scan > sales.floorplan
loss.patrol > sales.floorplan
inventory.update > receiving.restocking
