# pharmaceutical warehouse
people: Emma, Jack, Lisa, David, Olivia
## Receiving | Checks and logs incoming shipments. | why: ensures quality and traceability
a inspect: inspect incoming shipments
t manifest: shipment manifest
a log: log receipt in system
inspect > manifest
inspect >> log
## Storage | Stores raw materials and products safely.
t racks: storage racks
a place: place items in racks
t labels: storage labels
place > labels
## Inventory | Tracks inventory levels and expiry dates.
t inventory: inventory records
a count: count stock monthly
a check: check expiry dates
count > inventory
check > inventory
## Packing | Prepares orders for shipment.
t pallets: warehouse pallets
a pack: pack orders
t boxes: packing boxes
pack > boxes
pack > pallets
## Shipping | Prepares and sends out orders.
a load: load pallets
a seal: seal shipments
t manifest: shipping manifest
load > manifest
seal > manifest
## cross
receiving.log > inventory.inventory
storage.place > inventory.inventory
inventory.count >> packing.pack
inventory.check >> packing.pack
packing.pack >> shipping.load
packing.pallets > shipping.load
