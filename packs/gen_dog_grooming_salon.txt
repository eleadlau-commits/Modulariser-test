# dog grooming salon
people: Mia, Jake, Zoe, Sam, Olivia
## Reception | Manages client check-ins and check-outs.
t schedule: grooming schedule
a book: book appointments
a check: check in dogs
book > schedule
check > schedule
## Grooming | Provides the grooming services to dogs.
t tools: grooming tools
a wash: wash the dog
a dry: dry the dog
a trim: trim hair
wash >> dry
dry >> trim
trim > tools
## Sales | Sells products like shampoos and brushes to clients.
t inventory: product inventory
a sell: sell grooming products
a recommend: recommend products
sell > inventory
recommend > inventory
## Aftercare | Provides advice and care instructions post-grooming.
t instructions: aftercare instructions
a advise: advise on aftercare
a handover: handover the dog to the client
advise > instructions
handover > instructions
handover >> advise
## Cleaning | Cleans the salon and equipment post-grooming.
t equipment: grooming equipment
a clean: clean the salon
a disinfect: disinfect equipment
clean > equipment
disinfect > equipment
## cross
reception.book > grooming.wash
grooming.trim >> aftercare.handover
sales.recommend >> grooming.wash
aftercare.handover >> cleaning.clean
