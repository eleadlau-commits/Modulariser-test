# school library
people: Emma, Liam, Olivia, Noah
## Cataloging | Adds and updates records in the library catalog.
t database: library database
a scan: scan book barcodes
a update: update catalog records
scan > database
update > database
## Circulation | Manages lending and returning of books.
t checkout: checkout records
a lend: lend books to students
a return: accept book returns
lend > checkout
return > checkout
## Shelving | Ensures books are correctly placed on the shelves.
t location: shelf locations
a sort: sort books by category
a place: place books on shelves
sort > location
place > location
## Acquisitions | Orders new books and materials for the library.
t order: purchase orders
a select: select new books
a order: place orders with suppliers
select > order
order > order
## Maintenance | Performs regular maintenance and repairs on books.
t inventory: maintenance inventory
a inspect: inspect books for damage
a repair: repair damaged books
inspect > inventory
repair > inventory
## cross
cataloging.update > shelving.sort
circulation.return > shelving.place
acquisitions.order > cataloging.scan
maintenance.inspect > cataloging.update
shelving.place > circulation.checkout
