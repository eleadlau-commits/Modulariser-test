# driving school
people: Sarah, Mike, Lisa, Ryan, John
## Registration | Manages student enrollments and records. | why: students need to be registered to take lessons
t form: registration form
a verify: verify student IDs
a record: record student details
verify > form
record > form
## Training | Schedules and conducts driving lessons.
t schedule: lesson schedule
a teach: teach driving techniques
a practice: practice driving maneuvers
teach > schedule
practice > schedule
## Assessment | Evaluates student driving skills and readiness.
t checklist: assessment checklist
a test: conduct the driving test
a evaluate: evaluate student performance
test > checklist
evaluate > checklist
## Administration | Handles paperwork and compliance requirements.
t certification: driving certification form
a process: process student paperwork
a comply: ensure compliance with regulations
process > certification
comply > certification
## Coordination | Coordinates vehicles, instructors, and students.
t roster: instructor roster
a assign: assign instructors to students
a allocate: allocate vehicles
assign > roster
allocate > roster
## cross
registration.record > training.schedule
registration.record > assessment.test
training.teach >> assessment.evaluate
training.practice >> assessment.evaluate
administration.process > registration.form
administration.comply > registration.form
coordination.assign > training.teach
coordination.allocate > training.practice
