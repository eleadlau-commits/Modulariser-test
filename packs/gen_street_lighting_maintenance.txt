# street lighting maintenance
people: Alice, Bob, Carlos, Dana
## Inspection | Inspects lights and reports issues.
a check: visually check lights
t issues: issue report
a measure: measure brightness levels
check >> issues
measure >> issues
## Planning | Schedules maintenance based on inspection reports.
t schedule: maintenance schedule
a prioritize: prioritize issues
t resources: available resources
prioritize > schedule
prioritize > resources
## Repairs | Fixes the reported issues.
a replace: replace faulty bulbs
t parts: spare parts inventory
a tighten: tighten connections
replace > parts
tighten > parts
## Testing | Ensures the lights meet standards post-repair.
a test: test repaired lights
t standards: lighting standards
test > standards
## Reporting | Documents the maintenance work done.
t report: maintenance report
a document: document repairs made
document > report
## cross
inspection.check > planning.schedule
inspection.measure > planning.schedule
repairs.replace > testing.test
repairs.tighten > testing.test
testing.test >> reporting.document
