# coastguard station
people: Beth, Carlos, Dave, Emily
## Watch | Monitors the海域情况并记录船只活动。| why: 必须有人实时监控海域
a monitor: 监控雷达
a log: 记录船只活动
a alert: 发出紧急警报
monitor >> log
monitor >> alert
## Rescue | 计划并执行海上救援行动。
a plan: 制定救援计划
a launch: 启动救援船只
t gear: 救援装备
launch > gear
## Communications | 协调内部和外部的通信。
a contact: 联系其他海岸警卫队
a notify: 通知相关机构
t radio: 无线电设备
contact > radio
notify > radio
## Weather | 监测并报告天气状况。
a observe: 监测天气
a report: 报告天气
t forecast: 天气预报
observe >> report
report > forecast
## Logistics | 管理物资和设备。
a inventory: 管理库存
a maintain: 维护设备
t supplies: 物资清单
inventory > supplies
maintain > supplies
## cross
watch.monitor > rescue.plan
watch.alert > communications.notify
rescue.launch >> logistics.maintain
weather.report > logistics.inventory
communications.contact > watch.log
logistics.supplies > rescue.gear
