# auction house
people: Emma, Liam, Olivia, Noah, Ava
## Cataloging | Records detailed information about each item.
t description: item description
a photograph: take photographs
t provenance: item provenance
photograph > description
description > provenance
## Valuation | Estimates the value of each item.
a research: research market value
t estimate: value estimate
research >> estimate
## Marketing | Promotes the auction and attracts bidders.
t brochure: auction brochure
a advertise: place online and print ads
t schedule: auction schedule
advertise > brochure
advertise > schedule
## Auction | Conducts the sale of the items.
t lot: auction lot
a call: call the auction
t hammer: auction hammer
call > lot
call > hammer
## Settlement | Handles payments and transfers of ownership.
a collect: collect payment
t receipt: payment receipt
a transfer: transfer legal title
collect >> receipt
transfer > receipt
## cross
cataloging.description > valuation.estimate
valuation.estimate > marketing.brochure
marketing.schedule > auction.call
cataloging.description > auction.lot
settlement.collect > auction.hammer
settlement.transfer > valuation.estimate
