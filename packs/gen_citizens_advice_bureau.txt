# citizens advice bureau
people: Sarah, Mark, Lisa, David, Elizabeth
## Intake | Gathers initial information from visitors. | why: to understand the visitor's issue and direct them appropriately.
a greet: greet visitors
a assess: assess issues
t form: intake form
greet >> assess
assess > form
## Case Management | Manages the case from start to finish. | why: to ensure the visitor's issue is properly addressed.
t file: case file
a track: track case progress
a update: update file
track >> update
update > file
## Legal Research | Searches for relevant laws and advice. | why: to provide accurate and up-to-date advice to the visitor.
t books: legal reference books
a search: search databases
a consult: consult legal experts
search > books
consult >> search
## Advocacy | Represents the visitor in disputes or negotiations. | why: to have a strong advocate on the visitor's side.
t letter: advocacy letter
a represent: represent in meetings
a negotiate: negotiate on behalf
represent > letter
negotiate >> represent
## Community Outreach | Engages with the community to raise awareness. | why: to reach more potential visitors and educate them.
t flyer: outreach flyer
a speak: speak at events
a distribute: distribute materials
speak > flyer
distribute > flyer
## cross
intake.assess > case.file
case.track > legal.search
case.update > advocacy.represent
advocacy.represent >> case.update
community.speak >> intake.greet
community.distribute >> intake.greet
