# helicopter charter
people: Alex, Jamie, Sam, Casey
## Booking | Processes and confirms client bookings.
t reservation: reservation form
a confirm: confirm booking details
a schedule: schedule the flight
confirm > reservation
schedule > reservation
## Maintenance | Ensures the helicopter is airworthy.
t checklist: maintenance checklist
a inspect: conduct pre-flight inspection
t logbook: maintenance logbook
inspect > checklist
inspect > logbook
## Flight | Prepares and conducts the helicopter flight.
t itinerary: flight itinerary
a brief: brief the crew
a fly: conduct the flight
brief > itinerary
brief >> fly
## Billing | Calculates and processes client payments.
t invoice: billing invoice
a calculate: calculate total cost
a charge: charge the client
calculate > invoice
charge > invoice
## Support | Handles client queries and problems.
t contact: contact log
a assist: assist with client queries
a report: report any issues
assist > contact
report > contact
## cross
booking.schedule > flight.itinerary
maintenance.inspect >> flight.brief
billing.calculate > booking.confirm
support.report >> maintenance.logbook
flight.fly >> billing.charge
