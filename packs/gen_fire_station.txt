# fire station
people: Mike, Sarah, Tom, Lisa, Andy
## Dispatch | Receives and prioritizes emergency calls. | why: someone has to decide the order of response
a receive: receive emergency calls
a prioritize: prioritize calls
t log: call log
receive >> prioritize
prioritize > log
## Response | Prepares and deploys to emergencies.
t gear: firefighter gear
a equip: prepare gear
a travel: travel to location
equip >> travel
## Investigation | Investigates the cause and circumstances of fires.
t report: investigation report
a assess: assess damage
a interview: interview witnesses
assess >> report
interview >> report
## Training | Conducts regular training for staff.
t schedule: training schedule
a simulate: conduct simulations
a review: review training exercises
simulate >> review
review > schedule
## Maintenance | Ensures the upkeep and readiness of equipment.
t checklist: maintenance checklist
a inspect: inspect equipment
a repair: repair equipment
inspect >> repair
repair > checklist
## cross
dispatch.receive > response.equip
response.travel >> investigation.assess
investigation.interview >> training.review
maintenance.inspect > response.equip
maintenance.repair > response.equip
