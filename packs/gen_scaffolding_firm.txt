## Planning | Coordinates the logistics and materials for a scaffolding project. | why: ensures everything is ready for the work
t plan: job plan
a estimate: provide cost estimates
t materials: material list
estimate > plan
estimate > materials
## Construction | Builds the scaffolding structure on site.
a erect: erect scaffolding
t scaffolding: scaffolding materials
a install: install safety components
erect > scaffolding
erect >> install
## Inspection | Ensures the structure meets safety and quality standards.
t checklist: inspection checklist
a inspect: perform site inspection
a approve: approve the scaffold setup
inspect > checklist
inspect >> approve
## Dismantling | Safely removes the scaffolding structure after completion.
a dismantle: dismantle scaffolding
t storage: storage location for materials
a store: store dismantled materials
dismantle >> store
store > storage
## Documentation | Maintains records and reports for compliance and reference.
t report: inspection report
a record: record job details
t logs: work logs
record > logs
record > report
