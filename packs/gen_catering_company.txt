# catering company
people: Jamie, Lisa, Peter, Sarah, Michael
## Quoting | Provides quotes based on event details. | why: clients need to know the cost before committing
t estimates: cost estimates
a quote: create detailed quote
t menu: catering menu
quote > estimates
quote > menu
## Planning | Organizes logistics and staffing for events. | why: ensures the event runs smoothly
t schedule: event schedule
a allocate: allocate staff and equipment
t checklist: preparation checklist
allocate > schedule
allocate > checklist
## Procurement | Orders supplies and manages deliveries. 
t suppliers: supplier list
a order: place orders
a receive: receive deliveries
order > suppliers
order >> receive
## Preparation | Prepares food and sets up for the event. 
t menu: catering menu
a prep: prepare food
a setup: set up event space
prep > menu
setup > prep
## Service | Manages the event during the service period. 
t schedule: event schedule
a serve: serve food and drinks
a manage: manage customer interactions
serve > schedule
manage > schedule
## Cleanup | Cleans up after the event. 
t checklist: cleanup checklist
a clean: clean event space
a return: return equipment
clean > checklist
clean >> return
## cross
quoting.menu > preparation.menu
planning.schedule > service.schedule
procurement.receive > preparation.setup
preparation.setup > service.serve
service.serve > cleanup.clean
cleanup.return > procurement.suppliers
