# tv news desk
people: Sarah, Mark, Lisa, David
## Research | Gathers information and checks facts.
a gather: collect information
t sources: source list
a verify: verify facts
gather > sources
gather >> verify
## Scripting | Writes the scripts for the news anchors.
t script: news script
a write: write script
a edit: edit script
write >> edit
edit > script
## Scheduling | Determines the order and timing of segments.
t schedule: news schedule
a plan: plan segment order
a time: time segment slots
plan > schedule
time > schedule
## Reporting | Conducts interviews and reports live.
t report: field report
a interview: conduct interviews
a report: report live
interview >> report
report > report
## Graphics | Creates the visuals for the news.
t graphics: visual graphics
a design: create graphics
a animate: animate graphics
design >> animate
animate > graphics
## cross
research.gather > scripting.write
research.verify > scripting.edit
scheduling.plan > reporting.interview
scheduling.time > reporting.report
reporting.interview >> graphics.animate
scripting.write > scheduling.schedule
scripting.edit > scheduling.schedule
