# scrap metal yard
people: Mike, Sarah, Tom, Lisa, John
## Receiving | Inspects and logs incoming scrap loads.
t manifest: scrap manifest
a inspect: inspect scrap quality
a log: log scrap details
inspect >> log
log > manifest
## Sorting | Separates scrap into different categories.
t categories: category bins
a sort: sort scrap
a weigh: weigh sorted scrap
sort >> weigh
weigh > categories
## Processing | Prepares scrap for sale by cutting or baling.
t cutting: cutting machine
t baling: baling machine
a cut: cut scrap
a bale: bale scrap
cut > cutting
bale > baling
## Sales | Arranges the sale of processed scrap.
t bids: sales bids
a bid: evaluate sales bids
a sell: arrange sale
bid > bids
bid >> sell
## Transport | Organizes transportation for outgoing scrap.
t trucks: transport trucks
a load: load trucks
a deliver: deliver scrap
load > trucks
load >> deliver
## cross
receiving.manifest > sorting.categories
receiving.log > processing.cut
receiving.log > processing.bale
sorting.sort > processing.cut
sales.bid > transport.load
sales.sell > transport.deliver
