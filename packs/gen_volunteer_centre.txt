# volunteer centre
people: Sarah, David, Lisa, Tom, Robert
## Recruitment | Finds and interviews potential volunteers.
a advertise: post job ads for volunteers
a interview: conduct interviews
t candidates: list of candidates
advertise >> interview
interview > candidates
## Onboarding | Welcomes new volunteers and trains them.
a welcome: welcome volunteers
a train: train volunteers
t manual: training manual
welcome >> train
train > manual
## Scheduling | Matches volunteers to shifts and sends reminders.
a match: match volunteers to shifts
a remind: send reminders
t roster: schedule roster
match >> remind
match > roster
## Feedback | Collects and reviews feedback from volunteers and beneficiaries.
a collect: collect feedback forms
a review: review feedback
t feedback: feedback summary
collect >> review
review > feedback
## Support | Provides ongoing support to volunteers.
a assist: assist volunteers with issues
a mentor: mentor volunteers
t contact: contact list
assist > contact
mentor > contact
## cross
recruitment.candidates > onboarding.welcome
onboarding.train > scheduling.match
scheduling.roster > support.assist
feedback.review > recruitment.advertise
feedback.review >> support.mentor
