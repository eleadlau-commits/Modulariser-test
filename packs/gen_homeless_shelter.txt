# homeless shelter
people: Maria, John, Sarah, Alan
## Intake | Assesses and registers new arrivals. | why: to know who is entering and their needs
a assess: conduct initial assessment
t forms: intake forms
a register: register individuals
assess >> register
register > forms
## Health | Provides medical and mental health services. | why: to address health needs
a check: provide check-ups
t medication: medication list
a therapy: provide therapy sessions
check > medication
therapy >> check
## Housing | Allocates temporary shelter and monitors conditions. | why: to ensure safe shelter
t list: shelter list
a assign: assign shelter space
a inspect: inspect shelters
assign > list
inspect > list
## Support | Offers case management and life skills training.
a assist: provide case management
t plan: individual support plan
a train: conduct life skills training
assist > plan
train >> assist
## Community | Organizes activities and links to community resources.
a organize: organize events
t resources: community resource list
a connect: connect residents to resources
organize > resources
connect > resources
## cross
intake.register > housing.assign
health.check >> support.assist
health.therapy >> support.assist
housing.inspect >> health.check
support.train > community.connect
community.resources >> support.plan
