## Labor | Manages the progress of labor until delivery. | why: ensures safe delivery
a monitor: monitor labor progress

## Delivery | Assists in the actual birth of the baby. | why: essential for newborn delivery
a deliver: deliver baby
t instruments: delivery instruments
a evaluate: evaluate newborn health
deliver > instruments
evaluate > deliver
## Postpartum | Cares for the mother and newborn after birth. | why: necessary for recovery and health
a care: provide postpartum care
t vitals: maternal vitals
a bond: facilitate mother-newborn bonding
care > vitals
bond > care
## Discharge | Prepares the mother and baby for home. | why: ensures a safe transition
a prepare: prepare discharge paperwork
t guidelines: discharge guidelines
a instruct: instruct on home care
prepare > guidelines
instruct > prepare
