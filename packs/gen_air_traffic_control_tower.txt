# air traffic control tower
people: Alex, Priya, Ben, Mei, Tom
## Coordination | Manages the flow of aircraft into and out of the airspace.
a schedule: schedule takeoffs and landings
t flightplan: flight plan list
a communicate: communicate with pilots and ground staff
schedule > flightplan
communicate >> schedule
## Monitoring | Tracks the position and status of aircraft in real-time.
t radar: radar display
a track: track aircraft movements
t log: flight log
track > radar
track >> log
## Communication | Ensures clear and consistent communication with aircraft.
a transmit: transmit instructions to aircraft
t frequency: communication frequency list
a receive: receive messages from aircraft
transmit > frequency
receive > frequency
## Safety | Ensures all safety protocols are followed and enforced.
t checklist: safety checklist
a verify: verify aircraft adherence to rules
a alert: alert in case of emergency
verify > checklist
alert >> verify
## Navigation | Provides navigational guidance to aircraft.
a guide: provide navigational guidance
t route: flight route map
a update: update aircraft on position
guide > route
update > route
## cross
coordination.schedule > monitoring.track
coordination.communicate > communication.transmit
monitoring.track >> safety.verify
navigation.guide > communication.transmit
navigation.update > communication.receive
safety.alert >> navigation.guide
