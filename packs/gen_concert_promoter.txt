## Booking | Secures the acts for the event. | why: talent is essential to draw crowds
a scout: identify potential acts
t roster: current roster
a negotiate: negotiate contract terms
scout >> negotiate
negotiate > roster
## Venue | Finds and secures a suitable venue.
a scout: scout potential venues
t venue: venue contract
a negotiate: negotiate venue terms
scout >> negotiate
negotiate > venue
## Marketing | Promotes the event to attract attendees.
t ad: social media ad
a launch: launch promotional campaign
t tickets: ticket sale links
launch > ad
launch > tickets
## Ticketing | Manages ticket sales and distribution.
a setup: set up ticketing system
t tickets: ticket sale links
a track: track ticket sales
setup > tickets
setup >> track
## Logistics | Handles the physical aspects of the event.
t schedule: event schedule
a setup: setup stage and equipment
a manage: manage on-site staff
setup > schedule
setup >> manage
