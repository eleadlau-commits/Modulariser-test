# police station custody suite

## Intake | Processes individuals brought into custody.
a intake: intake new detainees
t forms: intake forms
a screen: screen for health and safety concerns
intake >> screen
intake > forms
## Holding | Monitors individuals in temporary custody.
t cells: holding cells
a monitor: monitor detainee behavior
a log: log detainee movements
monitor > log
log > cells
## Medical | Provides health care and support for detainees.
t infirmary: infirmary
a triage: triage medical issues
t supplies: medical supplies
triage > supplies
triage > infirmary
## Legal | Facilitates detainee communication with legal representatives.
t phones: legal phones
a contact: arrange legal contact
t access: legal access log
contact > access
contact > phones
## Release | Prepares detainees for release or transfer.
a prep: prepare release paperwork
t roster: release roster
a escort: escort detainee out
prep >> escort
prep > roster
