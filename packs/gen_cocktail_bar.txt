# cocktail bar
people: Lily, Max, Zoe, Sam, Finn
## Purchasing | Acquires ingredients and supplies. | why: necessary for cocktail preparation
a order: order ingredients
t suppliers: supplier list
a receive: receive deliveries
order > suppliers
order >> receive
## Stock | Manages inventory of ingredients and supplies.
t sheet: stock sheet
a count: count stock weekly
count > sheet
## Mixing | Prepares cocktails and garnishes. | why: core service of the bar
t recipe: cocktail recipe
a mix: mix cocktails
t garnish: garnish ingredients
mix > recipe
mix > garnish
## Serving | Serves cocktails and interacts with guests.
t menu: menu
a pour: pour cocktails
a serve: serve guests
pour > menu
serve > menu
## Cleaning | Cleans and prepares equipment and areas.
t checklist: cleaning checklist
a clean: clean equipment
t area: bar area
clean > checklist
clean > area
## cross
mixing.mix >> serving.pour
mixing.garnish > serving.serve
purchasing.receive > stock.sheet
stock.sheet > mixing.recipe
stock.sheet > cleaning.checklist
stock.sheet > serving.menu
