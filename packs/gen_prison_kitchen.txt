# prison kitchen
people: Ana, Raj, Mei, Tom
## Planning | Plans meals and schedules for the week. | why: meals need to be organized and scheduled
t menu: weekly menu
a schedule: create meal schedule
t ingredients: ingredient list
schedule > menu
schedule > ingredients
## Procurement | Orders and receives food supplies. | why: food needs to be ordered
a order: place order for ingredients
t suppliers: supplier list
a receive: check deliveries
order > suppliers
order >> receive
## Inventory | Manages the storage and stock of food items.
t stock: inventory list
a count: count stock monthly
count > stock
## Preparation | Prepares ingredients and portions meals. 
t prep: prep list
a prep: portion ingredients
a cook: cook meals
prep > prep
cook > prep
## Distribution | Distributes meals to different units and inmates.
t schedule: distribution schedule
a distribute: distribute meals
distribute > schedule
## Cleaning | Cleans kitchen equipment and prepares for next use.
t checklist: cleaning checklist
a clean: clean equipment
a sanitize: sanitize surfaces
clean > checklist
sanitize > checklist
## cross
planning.ingredients > procurement.order
planning.menu > preparation.cook
procurement.receive > inventory.stock
inventory.stock > preparation.prep
preparation.cook >> distribution.distribute
distribution.schedule > cleaning.clean
preparation.cook >> cleaning.clean
