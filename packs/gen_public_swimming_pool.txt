# public swimming pool
people: Laura, Sam, Ethan, Olivia, Liam
## Maintenance | Ensures the pool and facilities are clean and operational.
t schedule: maintenance schedule
a clean: clean pool and facilities
a inspect: inspect equipment
clean > schedule
inspect > schedule
## Lifeguarding | Ensures the safety of swimmers.
t roster: lifeguard roster
a patrol: patrol the pool area
a rescue: perform rescue operations
patrol > roster
rescue > roster
## Operations | Manages the day-to-day running of the pool.
t log: daily operations log
a open: open the pool
a close: close the pool
open > log
close > log
## Ticketing | Sells and controls entry to the pool.
t tickets: ticket records
a sell: sell tickets
a check: check tickets at entry
sell > tickets
check > tickets
## Facilities | Manages the use of additional facilities like lockers, showers, etc.
t availability: facility availability
a book: book facilities
a use: use facilities
book > availability
use > availability
## cross
maintenance.schedule > operations.log
lifeguarding.patrol > operations.log
ticketing.sell >> operations.open
ticketing.check >> facilities.use
facilities.use > ticketing.check
maintenance.clean >> ticketing.sell
