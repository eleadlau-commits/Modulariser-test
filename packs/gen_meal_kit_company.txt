# meal kit company
people: Lily, Sam, Emma, Jake
## Sourcing | Selects and orders ingredients from suppliers. | why: Ingredients are the core of the meal kit.
a select: select ingredients
t suppliers: supplier list
a order: place orders
select >> order
order > suppliers
## Preparation | Preps ingredients and assembles meal kits. | why: Makes the meal kit ready for delivery.
t prep: prep list
a prep: prepare ingredients
t kit: meal kit
prep > kit
prep > prep
## Quality | Ensures the quality of the meal kit. | why: Quality control is essential for customer satisfaction.
a inspect: inspect meal kits
a test: taste test meal kits
t checklist: quality checklist
inspect > checklist
test > checklist
## Packaging | Packages the meal kits for delivery. | why: Proper packaging ensures the meal kit arrives in good condition.
t box: packaging box
a pack: pack meal kit
a seal: seal box
pack > box
pack >> seal
## Delivery | Coordinates the delivery of the meal kits. | why: Ensures the meal kit reaches the customer.
t schedule: delivery schedule
a deliver: deliver meal kit
a track: track delivery
deliver > track
deliver > schedule
## cross
sourcing.order > preparation.prep
preparation.kit > quality.inspect
quality.checklist >> packaging.pack
packaging.seal > delivery.deliver
