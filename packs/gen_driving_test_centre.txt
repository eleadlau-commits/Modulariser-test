# driving test centre
people: Lisa, Mark, Anna, Carlos, David
## Registration | Manages the registration process for candidates.
t forms: registration forms
a verify: verify candidate information
a schedule: schedule the test
verify > forms
schedule > forms
## Theory Test | Conducts the theory test, evaluates the candidate's knowledge.
a administer: administer the theory test
t questions: question bank
a evaluate: evaluate the answers
administer > questions
administer >> evaluate
## Practical Test | Conducts the practical driving test.
a inspect: inspect the vehicle
a drive: drive with the candidate
t checklist: driving checklist
inspect >> drive
drive > checklist
## Assessment | Compiles the assessment results and issues the driving license.
a compile: compile the assessment
t license: driving license
a issue: issue the driving license
compile >> issue
issue > license
## Records | Maintains the records of all candidates and their test results.
t database: candidate database
a log: log test results
a update: update the records
log > database
update > database
## cross
registration.schedule > theory.administer
theory.evaluate > practical.inspect
practical.drive > assessment.compile
assessment.issue > records.log
records.update > registration.forms
