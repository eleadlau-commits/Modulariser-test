# pub
people: Emma, Liam, Olivia, Noah, Isabella
## Stock | Tracks and manages the inventory of drinks and supplies.
t sheet: stock sheet
a count: count inventory weekly
a order: place orders for stock
count > sheet
order > sheet
## Bar | Prepares and serves drinks to customers.
t menu: drink menu
a mix: mix drinks
a serve: serve drinks
mix > menu
serve > mix
## Front | Welcomes guests, takes bookings, and manages the seating area.
t bookings: booking book
a seat: seat guests
a greet: greet customers
seat > bookings
greet >> seat
## Billing | Handles payment and maintains the till.
a pay: take payment
t till: till report
a close: close the till
pay >> close
close > till
## Cleaning | Cleans the pub after hours and maintains hygiene.
t schedule: cleaning schedule
a sweep: sweep floors
a wipe: wipe surfaces
sweep > schedule
wipe > schedule
## cross
bar.mix > stock.sheet
front.seat > bar.serve
billing.pay > front.greet
stock.order > cleaning.schedule
cleaning.sweep > front.seat
