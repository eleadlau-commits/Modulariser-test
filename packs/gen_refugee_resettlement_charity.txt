## Initial Assessment | Evaluates the needs and background of incoming refugees.
a interview: conduct initial interviews
t file: case file
a assess: assess needs
interview >> assess
assess > file
## Legal Support | Provides legal resources and guidance for immigration.
t lawyer: legal counsel
a consult: consult with lawyer
a apply: apply for legal documents
consult > lawyer
consult >> apply
## Housing | Secures and prepares living spaces for incoming refugees.
t lease: housing lease
a inspect: inspect living space
a prepare: prepare living space
inspect >> prepare
prepare > lease
## Education | Identifies educational needs and connects refugees to resources.
t plan: education plan
a enroll: enroll in schools
a tutor: provide tutoring
enroll > plan
tutor > plan
## Employment | Assists refugees in finding job opportunities and training.
t resume: employment resume
a train: provide job training
a place: job placement
train >> place
place > resume
## cross
initial.assess > legal.apply
housing.prepare > initial.file
education.enroll >> employment.train
employment.place >> education.plan
