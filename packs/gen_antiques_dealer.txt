# antiques dealer
people: Sarah, John, Emily, Michael
## Sourcing | Finds and acquires antiques for the store.
a scout: scout for antiques
t inventory: list of items
a negotiate: negotiate prices
scout > inventory
scout >> negotiate
## Authentication | Verifies the authenticity and provenance of items.
a research: research provenance
a authenticate: authenticate item
t report: authentication report
research >> authenticate
authenticate > report
## Valuation | Determines the monetary value of antiques.
a appraise: appraise items
t appraisal: valuation report
a compare: compare market value
appraise >> compare
appraise > appraisal
## Display | Organizes and presents the antiques for retail.
t layout: display layout
a arrange: arrange items
a label: label items
arrange > layout
arrange >> label
## Sales | Sells antiques to customers.
a sell: sell items
t ledger: sales ledger
a record: record sale
sell >> record
record > ledger
## cross
sourcing.inventory > authentication.authenticate
authentication.report > valuation.appraise
valuation.appraise > display.arrange
display.arrange > sales.sell
valuation.appraise > sales.sell
