# university admissions office
people: Lisa, John, Maria, Tom
## Application | Collects and verifies applications. | why: applications need to be reviewed for admission
a receive: receive applications
t form: application form
a verify: verify documents
receive >> verify
## Evaluation | Reviews applications and scores them. | why: applications need to be scored for fairness
a review: review applications
t score: evaluation score
review >> score
## Selection | Selects candidates based on criteria. | why: a decision needs to be made on who gets admitted
a decide: decide admissions
t list: shortlist
decide > list
## Communication | Sends out acceptance or rejection letters. | why: candidates need to be informed of their status
a send: send letters
t template: letter template
send > template
## Registration | Registers admitted students and finalizes enrollment. | why: registration is needed to complete enrollment
a finalize: complete registration
t form: registration form
finalize > form
## cross
application.receive > evaluation.review
application.verify > evaluation.review
evaluation.score > selection.decide
selection.list > communication.send
communication.send > registration.finalize
registration.finalize > application.form
