# garden centre
people: Sarah, Mark, Lisa, John, Emma

## Stock Management | Tracks the inventory and ensures availability of plants and supplies. | why: keeps track of what's available to sell
t sheet: stock sheet
a count: count stock weekly
a reorder: reorder when stock is low
count > sheet
reorder > sheet

## Purchasing | Orders new stock and checks deliveries. | why: ensures new stock arrives
t suppliers: supplier list
a order: order new stock
a receive: receive deliveries
order > suppliers
order >> receive

## Nursery | Grows and maintains plants for sale.
t plants: plant list
a grow: grow plants
a care: care for plants
grow >> care
care > plants

## Sales | Handles customer transactions and maintains records. | why: ensures customers can purchase
t receipts: sales receipts
a sell: sell plants and supplies
a record: record sales
sell >> record
record > receipts

## Display | Sets up and maintains plant displays. | why: attracts customers and showcases products
t display: display plan
a arrange: arrange displays
a maintain: maintain displays
arrange > display
arrange >> maintain

## cross
stock.sheet > purchasing.suppliers
purchasing.receive > stock.sheet
nursery.care > stock.sheet
sales.sell > stock.sheet
display.arrange > nursery.plants
display.maintain > sales.sell
