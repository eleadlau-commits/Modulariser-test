# credit union
people: Emily, Jack, Sarah, David, Michael
## Loan Processing | Approves and disburses loans to members.
t application: loan application
a assess: assess creditworthiness
a approve: approve loan
a disburse: disburse funds
assess >> approve
approve >> disburse
disburse > application
## Deposit Services | Manages member deposits and savings accounts.
t account: deposit account
a open: open new accounts
a update: update account balances
open >> update
update > account
## Financial Planning | Advises on investment and savings plans for members.
t plan: financial plan
a consult: consult with members
a recommend: recommend investment options
consult >> recommend
recommend > plan
## Compliance | Ensures operations comply with financial regulations.
t audit: compliance audit
a review: review operations
a report: report findings
review >> report
report > audit
## Customer Service | Handles inquiries and transactions for members.
t inquiry: customer inquiry
a respond: respond to inquiries
a process: process transactions
respond > inquiry
process >> respond
## cross
loan.assess > compliance.audit
loan.disburse > deposit.account
deposit.update > financial.plan
financial.recommend >> customer.respond
compliance.report > loan.application
compliance.report > deposit.account
