# cricket club
people: Sarah, Jamie, Ryan, Lisa, Tom
## Membership | Manages the club's members and their subscriptions.
t members: list of members
a register: register new members
a renew: renew subscriptions
register > members
renew > members
## Training | Organizes training sessions for members.
t schedule: training schedule
a plan: plan training sessions
a conduct: conduct training sessions
plan > schedule
plan >> conduct
## Matches | Coordinates matches and schedules them.
t fixtures: match fixtures
a arrange: arrange matches
a schedule: schedule matches
arrange > fixtures
arrange >> schedule
## Equipment | Manages the club's equipment and ensures it is available for use.
t inventory: equipment inventory
a check: check equipment
a maintain: maintain equipment
check > inventory
maintain > inventory
## Finance | Manages the club's finances, including membership fees and expenses.
t accounts: financial accounts
a collect: collect fees
a pay: pay expenses
collect > accounts
pay >> collect
## cross
membership.register > finance.collect
membership.renew > finance.collect
training.schedule > matches.fixtures
matches.schedule >> training.conduct
equipment.check > training.conduct
equipment.maintain > matches.schedule
finance.accounts > equipment.maintain
finance.accounts > membership.renew
