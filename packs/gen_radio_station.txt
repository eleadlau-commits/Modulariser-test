# radio station
people: Linda, Sam, Mia, Alex, Ben
## Programming | Creates and schedules the daily radio content. | why: content must be organized and scheduled
t playlist: daily playlist
a schedule: schedule shows and advertisements
t log: program log
schedule > playlist
schedule > log
## Production | Produces live and recorded content. 
a record: record voiceovers and interviews
a live: conduct live broadcasts
t studio: recording studio
record > studio
live > studio
## Advertising | Sells and places advertisements. | why: stations need revenue
t adlist: advertisement list
a sell: sell advertising slots
a place: place ads in the schedule
sell >> adlist
place > adlist
## Technical Operations | Supports the transmission and technical aspects of broadcasting. 
a monitor: monitor transmission quality
a maintain: maintain equipment
t transmitter: main transmitter
monitor >> transmitter
maintain > transmitter
## Promotion | Manages social media and community relations. 
t posts: social media posts
a post: publish posts
a engage: engage with community
post > posts
engage > posts
## cross
programming.log > technical.transmitter
programming.schedule > production.live
production.record > programming.playlist
advertising.adlist > programming.schedule
promotion.post >> programming.log
